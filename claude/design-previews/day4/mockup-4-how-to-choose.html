[ How to choose ]

How do you choose a GEO provider?

Five questions to ask any provider, including us.

Do they show the prompts they test, and the full answers?

AI answers change from run to run. A provider who shows only a screenshot is showing one sample.

Do they report the share of runs you appear in, not a "rank in AI"?

Published research found the order of brands in AI answers is close to random. Appearance across many runs is the fair measure.

Does every figure come with a named source and a date?

Ask where each statistic comes from. If it is a vendor survey, they should say so.

Do they fix access and indexing before they write anything?

Pages that cannot be crawled or indexed cannot be cited. This step is cheap and comes first.

Do they tell you what they cannot control?

No one controls what an AI tool writes. Honest providers say so and show how they measure.
