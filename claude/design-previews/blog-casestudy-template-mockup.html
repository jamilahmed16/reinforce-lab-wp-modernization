In short

Most product pages were blocked from Google by a template setting. We fixed the template, rebuilt the site structure, and wrote disease-area pages with medical review built in. Indexed pages rose from 140 to 610 in six months.

1The brief

The challenge

The client's site had grown to more than 700 pages, but Google showed fewer than 150. Enquiries from healthcare professionals had stalled, and every page change needed medical and legal sign-off, which slowed fixes down.

Honest notes

What did not go to plan

Problem

Medical review took three weeks per page at first, so publishing fell behind.

How we handled it

We booked review slots a month ahead and sent pages in batches of four.

Problem

Merging duplicate pages broke links in old email campaigns.

How we handled it

We added redirects for every old URL and checked the top 200 links by hand.

3The outcome

What changed

Within six weeks of the template fix, Google had indexed most of the blocked pages. The new disease-area pages started bringing in searches from clinicians, and the review workflow meant no page was sent back by the client's compliance team.

What's next

Ongoing work

Monthly technical checks, six more disease-area pages each quarter, and tracking how AI tools describe the client's products.

Lessons

What made the difference

01

Find the cause first

One template setting blocked hundreds of pages. Fixing it did more than any amount of new content.

02

Plan review into the work

Booking medical review slots up front kept publishing on schedule.

03

Structure by therapy area

Grouping pages the way clinicians search helped Google and readers find them.
