What is generative engine optimization?

Generative Engine Optimization

(GEO)
nounAlso called AI search optimization, LLM optimizationField Search marketing
JJamil AhmedPublished 12 Sep 2026Updated 2 Oct 2026

Definition

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of making your content easy for AI answer tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews to find, trust and cite, so your brand appears inside the answers they write.

Reinforce Lab definition. The term comes from a 2023 research paper by Aggarwal and others.

Three levels

GEO explained

10 secThe idea

SEO gets you ranked in search results. GEO gets you quoted in AI answers.

1 minThe basics

AI answer tools read many pages and write one answer, often with a few sources cited. GEO makes your pages the ones they pick: clear facts near the top, claims backed by sources, and your brand described the same way across the web.

In depthThe full article

The sections below cover how AI tools choose sources, what GEO changes on a page, and how to measure it. Read on ↓

How it works

From question to cited answer

Question

Someone asks an AI tool a question in plain words.

Retrieval

The tool searches for pages that match and reads the useful passages.

Selection

It keeps passages that are clear, specific and backed by sources.

Answer

It writes one answer and cites a few of the pages it used.

GEO works on the middle two steps. If your page is not found, or its key facts are buried, the tool cites someone else. That is why GEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it: a page that is never crawled can never be cited.

Clearing it up

What GEO is and is not

GEO is

  • Writing facts AI tools can lift and quote
  • Backing claims with sources and data
  • Describing your brand the same way everywhere

GEO is not

  • A replacement for SEO
  • Stuffing pages with questions and keywords
  • Something one tool or plugin can do for you

Questions

FAQs

Is GEO the same as SEO?

No. SEO gets pages ranked in search results; GEO gets content cited inside AI answers. GEO depends on SEO, because AI tools mostly cite pages they can find.

How do you measure GEO?

Track how often AI tools mention or cite your brand for the questions your buyers ask, and compare it with your competitors over time.
