Which sources AI search tools cite when B2B buyers ask for help

We asked three AI answer tools 400 questions that B2B buyers ask before they choose a supplier, and recorded every source each answer cited.

Sample data

In short

AI answer tools cite vendor websites more often than many marketers expect, most of all for pricing and shortlist questions. The pages they cite are mostly guides and comparison pages that answer the question in the first paragraph, not product pages.

Results

Key findings

Sample data
  1. 61%Finding 1

    Six in ten answers cited at least one vendor website.

    Industry publications came second (48%) and review sites third (39%). See Figure 1

  2. 81%Finding 2

    Pricing questions cited a vendor website most often.

    Definition questions cited one least often (38%). See Figure 2

  3. 2.1×Finding 3

    Pages that answer the question in the first 100 words were cited 2.1 times as often.

    Compared with pages on the same topic that put the answer further down.

  4. 27%Finding 4

    Only 27% of cited vendor pages were product or service pages.

    The rest were guides, comparison pages and pricing pages.

  5. 3.4Finding 5

    Each answer cited 3.4 sources on average.

    One in five answers cited no source at all.

The data

What we found

Vendor websites were the most cited source type across all three tools. That held for every question type except definitions, where reference sites and industry publications led.

The vendor pages that were cited had one thing in common: they answered the buyer's question in plain words near the top, then backed it up with detail. Product pages written as sales copy were rarely cited, even for questions about that product type.

Method

How we ran the study

Sample
400 questions, 3 tools, 1,200 answers
Period
1 to 31 July 2026
Sources
Answers saved as screenshots and text
Analysis
Each citation classified twice; disagreements settled by a third person
  1. 1

    Write the questions

    400 questions from sales calls, Search Console and forums, across 8 industries.

  2. 2

    Collect the answers

    Each question asked once in each tool, logged out, from a UK or US location.

  3. 3

    Classify the citations

    Every cited source put into one of six types by two people working apart.

  4. 4

    Analyse

    Shares calculated per answer, then split by question type and industry.

Definitions
Cited
The answer shows a link to the source or names it.
Vendor website
The website of a company that sells the service in question.
Question type
Definition, how-to, comparison, shortlist or pricing, set before collection.
Limits of this study
  • AI answers change from day to day. We asked each question once per tool.
  • Questions were in English, for UK and US buyers only.
  • We measured citations, not clicks or sales.
  • Three tools only. Results may differ in others.
