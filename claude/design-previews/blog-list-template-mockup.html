Short answer

Northwind Host is the best managed WordPress host for most business sites: it was the fastest in our tests and its support knew WordPress. Copperline is the best value, and Tidewell Cloud is the pick for high-traffic sites.

Cheap shared hosting is fine for a hobby blog. For a site that brings in enquiries, slow pages and downtime cost you leads. Managed WordPress hosts handle updates, backups and caching for you, and they differ more than their sales pages suggest.

Questions

FAQs

Is managed WordPress hosting worth the extra cost?

For a site that brings in leads or sales, usually yes. You pay for speed, backups and support that knows WordPress, which saves time when something breaks.

Can I move hosts without losing rankings?

Yes, if every URL stays the same and the new host is at least as fast. Check speed and Search Console for a few weeks after the move.
