About the agentFree diagnostic

In short

The agent pulls every important claim out of a draft, matches each one to a source, scores how strong the evidence is, and sends anything weak or conflicting to a person. Your reviewers keep the final say.

Run the agent on a draft before it goes to medical-legal review. Reviewers get a claim-by-claim evidence log instead of hunting for sources, and only the flagged claims need their time.

Version 0.3 checks medical claims against PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov and sends weak evidence to a named reviewer. It still does not approve content on its own.

The problem

What goes wrong without it

Unsupported claims

Statistics and statements with no source, or a source that does not say it.

Outdated facts

Figures that were true years ago, still published today.

Risk in regulated fields

In health, finance and law, one wrong claim can cost more than the page earns.

Step by step

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Extract claims

    Every statistic, fact and statement that needs support.

    Out: a list of claims
  2. Step 2

    Match sources

    Each claim linked to the source that supports it.

    Out: claim and source pairs
  3. Step 3

    Score confidence

    How strong, recent and relevant the evidence is.

    Out: a score per claim
  4. Step 4

    Flag for review

    Weak, conflicting or missing evidence sent to a person.

    A person decides
    Out: the evidence log
What goes inA draft page, the sources you already use, and the domain databases your field relies on.
What comes outA claim-by-claim evidence log, confidence scores, review flags and suggested corrections.

The situation

Where this helps

A pharmaceutical content team publishes four pages a month. Every page goes to medical-legal review, and reviewers spend most of their time finding the source behind each claim. Pages wait weeks in the queue.

Before you start

What you need

  • The drafts, in one shared place
  • The sources your team trusts, as a list
  • A named reviewer for each therapy area
  • Your house rules for claims and wording

Honest limits

What it does and does not do

It does
  • Find a source for every important claim
  • Score how strong the evidence is
  • Flag weak or conflicting evidence
It does not
  • Approve content on its own
  • Replace medical, legal or compliance review
  • Judge tone, brand or style

A confidence score is not a sign-off. Anything the agent flags goes to a person, and in regulated fields your own reviewers keep the final say.

Fit

Who it is for

A good fit if
  • You publish in health, finance or law
  • Review slows your publishing down
  • You need an audit trail for claims
Not the right tool if
  • You need content written, not checked
  • Your pages make few factual claims

Questions

FAQs

Does it replace our medical or legal review?

No. It prepares an evidence log that makes that review faster and more thorough. Final approval stays with your reviewers.

Which sources does it use?

Official and primary sources first, and domain databases such as PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov for medical and life-sciences content.
