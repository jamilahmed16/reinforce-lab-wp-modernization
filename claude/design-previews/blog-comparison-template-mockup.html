Short answer

An agency is usually the better start for companies spending under the cost of two full-time specialists, because you get every skill at once. An in-house team wins once SEO drives a large share of revenue and you need someone inside every product decision.

Most companies ask this question after SEO has started to matter, but before they know what it needs. The answer depends less on which option is better in general and more on your budget, how fast you need results, and how close SEO sits to your product.

Our verdict

Start with an agency if you are building SEO from scratch or need a full set of skills fast. Move to an in-house team, or a lead plus an agency, once SEO drives enough revenue to justify specialists on the payroll.

Questions

FAQs

Is an agency cheaper than an in-house SEO?

Usually, until you need more than one or two specialists full time. Compare the full cost of each hire, including tools and training, with the retainer.

Can I switch from an agency to in-house later?

Yes. Ask the agency for full access to every account and document from the start, so the handover is simple.
