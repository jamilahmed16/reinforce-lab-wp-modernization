    Short answer

    AI search optimization is the work of making AI tools describe your business correctly and cite your pages when buyers ask about your category. It rests on normal SEO, then adds clear facts, quotable passages and consistent profiles across the web.

    01

    Chapter 1 · 4 min

    How AI answers are built

    When someone asks an AI search tool a question, it does not invent the answer from nothing. It looks for pages that match the question, reads the most useful passages and writes a short answer, often with links to the pages it used.

    That means your pages compete twice: once to be found, and again to be the passage the tool chooses to quote. A page can rank well and still never be cited, because its best information is buried or vague.

    Ranking gets you considered. A clear passage gets you cited.

    Google says there are no extra technical requirements to appear in AI Overviews beyond what it asks of any page in Search. The page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet. The rest is the quality and clarity of what you publish.

    02

    Chapter 2 · 4 min

    Why your facts must agree everywhere

    AI tools learn who you are from many places: your website, your structured data, business profiles, directories, reviews and press coverage. When those sources disagree, the tool has to guess, and it may guess wrong.

    Start with one source of truth

    Write down the name, description, services, locations and key facts you want every AI tool to repeat. Then make your About page, your Organization schema and your main profiles say exactly that.

    • One business name, written the same way everywhere
    • One short description of what you do and who you serve
    • The same address and phone number on every listing
    TipWhen an AI tool describes you wrongly, check old directory listings and any profile that was not updated after a rebrand or move.

    Questions

    FAQs

    Do I need a separate strategy for each AI tool?

    No. The foundations are shared: crawlable pages, clear facts and consistent profiles. You then check each tool on the same set of questions.

    How long until AI answers change?

    It varies by tool and topic. Changes to pages that tools fetch live can show up sooner than changes to what a model learned in training.
