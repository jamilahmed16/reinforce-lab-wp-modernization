AEO stands for answer engine optimization. In marketing, AEO is the practice of making your content and brand easy for answer engines, such as Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, to understand, trust and use in their answers. The goal moves from "get the click" to "be named and cited when a buyer asks a question." It builds on SEO. It does not replace it.

This guide gives a plain definition, shows how AEO differs from SEO and GEO, and explains why it matters for marketers. It also covers how to use content marketing for AEO, a seven-step plan, how to measure it and a worked example. We cite only sources we could read at the publisher, and we say when a figure is a survey, a forecast or one vendor's data.

What does AEO stand for in marketing?

AEO means answer engine optimization. An answer engine is a tool that gives a direct answer to a question, instead of only a list of links. Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT search, Gemini, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot are examples. Voice assistants and featured snippets work in a similar way. So when people ask "what is AEO in digital marketing", the answer is this: it is the work of earning a place in those answers.

You may also see "what is AEO and GEO in marketing". GEO stands for generative engine optimization. The two terms overlap a great deal. AEO is often used for being the direct answer, including in snippets and voice. GEO is often used for being cited by generative AI tools. Many teams do one body of work and use both names. Our guide to AEO vs SEO and our guide to GEO vs SEO cover the details.

What is the meaning of AEO? A short definition

AEO means answer engine optimization. It is the practice of shaping your content so that search engines and AI tools can take it as the direct answer to a question. In digital marketing, that means a clear answer first, then proof, then structure that a machine can read.

The same letters mean something else in trade. In customs, AEO stands for Authorized Economic Operator, a status for trusted traders. If you searched for "AEO certification," that is a different subject. This guide covers the marketing meaning.

What is AEO and GEO in marketing?

GEO means generative engine optimization. AEO aims to make your page the answer. GEO aims to get your brand cited or named in answers that AI tools write. Marketers often do both with the same pages. Read our guides to AEO vs SEO and GEO vs SEO for the differences.

How is AEO different from SEO?

Forrester describes AEO as significantly, but not fundamentally, different from SEO. Both shape what people see on and off your site, both span content and technical work, and both are hard to measure. That is a fair summary. The table shows the main differences in daily work.

AreaSEOAEO
GoalRankings and clicksBeing named, cited and described correctly in answers

Content shapeA page that matches intentA page with a direct answer, then proof and detail

Google takes a plain view. In its guide to generative AI features, it says that optimizing for generative AI search is optimizing for the search experience, and so it is still SEO. It advises care with third-party "AEO" or "GEO" advice.

Why does AEO matter for marketers?

Three kinds of evidence point the same way. They differ in quality, so we state the limits.

  • Buyers use AI to research. G2 surveyed 1,169 B2B decision-makers in April 2025. It found that 79% said AI search had changed how they research, and 29% said they start with AI search more often than Google. Generative AI chatbots were the top source influencing vendor shortlists, at 17.1%. The sample is software buyers who answered G2's survey, and G2 sells software review services.
  • Clicks fall when an AI summary appears. The Pew Research Center tracked 900 US adults in March 2025. People clicked a regular result in 8% of visits to pages with an AI summary and 15% of visits to pages without one, and clicked a link inside the summary in 1% of visits. Google disputes the study's method.
  • A forecast says search volume will fall. In February 2024, Gartner predicted that traditional search engine volume would drop 25% by 2026, with search marketing losing share to AI chatbots and other virtual agents. It is a forecast, not a measurement. We did not find a source that confirms the figure, so treat it as a view that was current when it was made.

The practical lesson is that being named in the answer matters, even when no click follows. That is why AEO measures mentions and citations as well as traffic.

How do answer engines choose what to cite?

The companies do not publish exact rules. What they say is limited. OpenAI says ChatGPT ranks search results using multiple factors meant to help people find relevant, reliable information, and that placement is not guaranteed. Google says its AI features use query fan-out, where one question is split into related searches, and that a page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet to be a supporting link. From the documents we read, three things seem to matter. This is our reading and not a formula:

  • Access: the page can be crawled, indexed and read in the HTML.
  • Fit: the page answers the question, or a related one, in a passage that stands alone.
  • Trust: the facts are clear and sourced, the author is named, and other sites agree with what you say.

Content marketing for AEO

Content marketing is where most teams do their AEO work. The shift is small in method and large in discipline. Start from questions, not topics, write the answer first, prove it, and keep it current. Here is how to adapt a normal content program.

Start with a question map

List 20 to 30 questions your buyers ask, from sales calls, support tickets, review sites, Search Console queries and "People also ask". Group them by stage: awareness ("what is X"), consideration ("X vs Y", "best X for Z") and decision ("how much does X cost", "how do I switch"). Mark which ones your site already answers well, and which it does not.

Choose the content types that answer questions

Content typeAnswersAEO tip
Definition page or glossary entry"What is [term]?"A one or two sentence definition first, then detail
How-to guide"How do I [task]?"Numbered steps and a short summary at the top
Comparison page"[A] vs [B]", "best [category]"A dated table and a verdict, with honest limits
Pricing and cost page"How much does it cost?"State prices, or the factors that set them
FAQ sectionCommon follow-upsUse real questions in the buyer's words
Original data or researchQuestions with no good source yetState the method, sample and limits
Case study"Does it work for someone like me?"Dates, a baseline and what else changed

Write for people first

Put the answer in the first sentence under each question heading, then add context, examples and sources. Keep paragraphs short and give each section one idea. Google warns that making separate pages for every small variation of a question, mainly to manipulate rankings or AI answers, breaks its scaled content abuse policy. So build fewer, better pages, and answer related questions inside them.

Add evidence

A Princeton-led team tested nine content changes on a 10,000-query benchmark. It reported that adding citations, quotations from relevant sources and statistics lifted visibility by up to 40%. That is a 2024 benchmark, and results varied by domain. It still supports a good habit: name the source, the date and the method for every number.

Refresh and repeat

Ahrefs analyzed 17 million citations across seven AI search platforms and found that content cited in AI answers was 25.7% fresher than content in regular results. That is a correlation from one vendor's data. Still, review key pages each quarter, fix facts that changed and show a real update date.

A seven-step AEO plan

  1. Check access. Make sure the search crawlers you want, such as Googlebot, Bingbot and OAI-SearchBot, are not blocked by robots.txt or your firewall. Our robots.txt guide explains the file.
  2. Run a baseline. Test your question list in several AI tools, three runs each, and record whether you are named, cited and described correctly.
  3. Fix your facts. Make your name, description, prices and locations match on your site and in directories.
  4. Write or rewrite the key pages with answer-first openings, clear headings and sourced evidence.
  5. Build real presence on other sites. Run your prompts, list the sites the answers cite, and ask how you can appear on them honestly, with profiles, reviews and useful contributions.
  6. Measure. Repeat the test monthly, and use free reports from Google and Bing.
  7. Refresh. Update pages when facts change, and review the plan each quarter.

How do you measure AEO?

No single number captures it. Use a small set of signs:

  • Mention and citation rate across a fixed prompt list. SparkToro had 600 volunteers run 12 prompts through ChatGPT, Claude and Google's AI features 2,961 times, and found less than a 1 in 100 chance of getting the same list of brands twice. So run each prompt several times and report a rate, not a rank.
  • Google's Generative AI performance report in Search Console, which shows impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools' AI Performance report, which shows citations in Copilot and Bing's AI summaries. Bing says its grounding queries are a sample.
  • Branded search in Search Console, which may rise when you are named without a link.
  • Leads and sales from pages you updated, tracked in analytics.

What does AEO not require?

Much AEO advice online has no source. Google says that for Google Search you do not need llms.txt files or other special files, "chunking" content into tiny pieces, rewriting content just for AI, or special structured data. It also says no third-party tool has access to its internal ranking or AI systems. This is Google's view of Google Search, and other tools may behave differently. Do the basics first, and test any extra step before you spend on it.

Worked example: a three-person B2B marketing team

This is an illustration, not a client result. Imagine a team that sells HR software to mid-sized companies. It builds a map of 24 questions from sales calls. Its site answers six well, answers nine poorly and does not answer nine.

The team tests the 24 questions three times in three tools, 216 answers in all, and is named in 14. The answers cite two review sites and a competitor's comparison page. The team picks six questions, including "how much does HR software cost" and "[competitor] alternative", and writes or rewrites six pages with answer-first openings, dated tables and two sourced statistics each. It also completes its profiles on both review sites. After 60 days it repeats the same 216 runs and compares the rate of mentions, then looks at the demo requests from the six pages. The result may be small or large. The plan makes it readable.

In every case, start with the questions your own buyers ask. The test is whether a stranger who asks an AI tool that question would be told about you, and told the truth.

Which AEO tools help?

You can begin with a spreadsheet and free tools: the AI tools themselves, Search Console's Generative AI performance report, Bing Webmaster Tools and your analytics. SEO suites such as Ahrefs and Semrush offer AI visibility features, including prompt research, and stand-alone trackers run prompts on a schedule. Run a hand test first, so you can judge whether a tool's numbers are right, and be careful with any tool that reports a precise rank inside AI answers. How long does AEO take, and what does it cost?

We found no independent data on typical timelines or fees. Access and fact fixes can change retrieval-based answers within weeks, while new content, reviews and outside mentions take longer. The cost depends on how many questions you cover, how much content you write, and whether you need technical fixes and outreach. If you hire help, compare proposals on deliverables, not on the monthly figure, and be wary of promises of placement. Nobody controls AI answers.

Does structured data matter for AEO?

Vendor guides often list FAQ, how-to and article markup as AEO tactics. Google's position is narrower. It says structured data is not required for its generative AI features and that no special schema.org markup is needed, though it still helps with rich result eligibility and clear facts. So use markup that matches your visible content, validate it, and do not expect it to earn a citation by itself. Put the effort first into the answer, the evidence and the facts. Then keep important pages fresh: Ahrefs found that content cited in AI answers was 25.7% fresher than content in regular results, a correlation from one vendor's data, and the practical step is to review key pages on a schedule and show a real update date.

What can you do this week?

You do not need a big program to begin. In one week, you can do five things. On day one, write down 15 questions your buyers ask. On day two, run each three times in ChatGPT and Google, and save the answers in a sheet. On day three, check your robots.txt and firewall for blocks on search crawlers. On day four, compare your name, description and prices across your site and your top three profiles, and fix the mismatches. On day five, pick the one question where you lose most clearly, and rewrite the page that should answer it, with the answer in the first sentence and two sourced facts. Then repeat the same test in a month. That small loop teaches more than any report.

What are the common mistakes?

  • Treating AEO as a separate trick. Most of it is good SEO with clearer answers and evidence.
  • Making a page for every phrasing. Google calls mass pages made to manipulate results scaled content abuse.
  • Using unsourced numbers. A figure with no source is a risk to readers and to models.
  • Chasing a rank in AI answers. Lists change from run to run.
  • Ignoring other sites. Answers often rely on reviews, lists and directories.
  • Testing once. One answer is a clue, not a result.

Final thoughts

AEO in marketing is the discipline of being the clear, trusted answer. Start with your buyers' questions, write the answer first, prove it with sources, keep facts consistent and current, and earn honest mentions on the sites that answers cite. Test with a fixed prompt list and expect to adjust.
