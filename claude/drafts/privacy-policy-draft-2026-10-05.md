# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [date of publishing]

This policy explains what information Reinforce Lab collects when you visit reinforcelab.com, fill in one of our forms or email us, what we do with it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and specific: if something here is unclear, email us at hello@reinforcelab.com.

## Who we are

Reinforce Lab Limited is the company responsible for your information (the "data controller"). Our registered office is Suite #1402, Level-13, Concord Tower, 113 Kazi Nazrul Islam Avenue, Dhaka 1000, Bangladesh. We also work from 2511 Pines Pointe Dr, Katy, TX 77493, USA, through Reinforce Lab Inc. `[CONFIRM: legal name of the US company and whether it shares responsibility for US enquiries]`

Contact for anything about privacy: **hello@reinforcelab.com**.

## What this policy covers

This policy covers our website, our forms and the emails you send us. When you become a client, the information we handle for you (for example access to your analytics or website) is covered by our agreement with you, not by this page.

## What we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Contact form:** your name, email address, and, if you choose, your company, website, the topic and your message.
- **Search Authority Diagnostic and SEO & AI Search Audit request form:** your name, work email and company, and, if you choose, your website, role, industry, biggest search challenge, monthly content volume and desired outcome.
- **Emails and calls:** whatever you choose to tell us when you email or phone us.

Please do not send us sensitive information (for example health information) through the forms.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Visit statistics:** we use Jetpack Stats, a service from Automattic, to count visits and see which pages are read. Your browser connects to Automattic's servers, which receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. We see totals, not who you are.
- **Fonts:** our pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser information.
- **Spam protection:** when you send a form, we keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address for one hour to limit repeated submissions. It is deleted automatically.
- **Server logs:** like every website, our hosting provider records technical requests (IP address, date and time, page requested) to keep the site running and secure. `[CONFIRM: hosting provider and how long its logs are kept]`

## Cookies

Our website does not set cookies for visitors. If you log in to manage the site (our team only), WordPress uses cookies to keep you logged in. If we ever add analytics or other tools that use cookies, we will update this page first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent. `[CONFIRM: will Google Analytics 4 or any other tracking be added at launch? If yes, this section and a consent banner are needed]`

## How we use your information

| What we do | Why we are allowed to |
|---|---|
| Reply to your message or request | You asked us to (steps before a possible contract), and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries |
| Prepare your Search Authority Diagnostic or audit proposal | You asked us to |
| Follow up about your request | Our legitimate interest in following up an enquiry you made; you can tell us to stop at any time |
| Keep the website working, secure and free of spam | Our legitimate interest in running a safe website |
| Understand which pages are useful | Our legitimate interest in improving the website, using totals only |
| Meet legal and tax obligations if you become a client | Legal obligation |

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not add you to a newsletter unless you ask to join one.

## International transfers

Our team works in Bangladesh and the United States, and our service providers may process information in other countries. When information moves between countries, we rely on our providers' standard safeguards, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses where they apply.

## How long we keep it

- Form submissions and emails that do not lead to work: up to 24 months, then deleted. `[CONFIRM]`
- Information about clients: for as long as we work together, then as long as tax and legal rules require. `[CONFIRM]`
- Spam-protection records: one hour.

## How we protect it

The site uses HTTPS. Form submissions are stored as private records that only our team can open, and access is limited to the people who need it. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, but we work to protect yours and will tell you and the authorities about a serious breach when the law requires it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

- tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- stop or limit how we use it, or object to a use based on our legitimate interests;
- send it to you or another company in a common format;
- withdraw consent you gave, at any time.

To make a request, email **hello@reinforcelab.com**. We will reply within one month and may ask you to confirm who you are. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

**If you live in the United States:** you can ask what we collect and ask us to delete it, as above. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

## Children

Our website and services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you think a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

## Links to other websites

Our pages link to other sites, such as LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Clutch and the sources we cite. Their privacy policies apply when you visit them.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will say so on this page.
