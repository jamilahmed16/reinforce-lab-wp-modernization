Namecheap and GoDaddy are two of the best-known names in domains and hosting. Both sell cheap shared hosting. This page compares what each says on its own pages and what Cybernews measured, as of 15 November 2026. We did not test either host ourselves.

Namecheap is cheaper in year one and at renewal on the entry plans. GoDaddy's entry plan is NVMe storage with 1 site. Namecheap offers 3 sites. GoDaddy's page lists a free domain on its plans. We did not find a free domain on the Namecheap page we read.

Neither host has a Black Friday hosting sale that we could confirm. Namecheap's Black Friday page refers to 2025, and GoDaddy's hosting page shows no Black Friday offer.

What about AI features?

Namecheap lists AI for WordPress and an AI website builder on every shared plan. The GoDaddy hosting plan section we read lists no AI features. We did not test any of them.

Which should you pick?

Pick Namecheap if price matters most and you want 3 sites on the entry plan. Pick GoDaddy if you want the stronger test numbers and a free domain, and you can commit to a 3-year term. If you mostly need a domain, remember that Namecheap is better known as a registrar than as a host.
