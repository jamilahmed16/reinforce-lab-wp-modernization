What is the DigitalOcean signup credit?

When you create your first team, DigitalOcean applies a $5 credit on its own. The credit expires 90 days after signup. It works on all products except SaaS Add-Ons. DigitalOcean's page says no promo code is required.

What can $5 buy?

The cheapest Basic Droplet is $4 a month, with 512 MiB RAM, 1 vCPU and 10 GiB SSD. The $5 credit covers about one month of it. A 1 GiB Droplet is $6 a month. This is enough to try the service, not to run a real site for long. Backups cost extra.
