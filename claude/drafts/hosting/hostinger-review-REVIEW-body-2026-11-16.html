Hostinger is one of the cheapest ways to get a website online. A 48-month plan starts at $1.99 a month, and AI tools come with it. The catch is the renewal price. Here is what you get, what you pay after the sale, and who should pick it.

What do you really pay for Hostinger?

The sale price needs a 48-month term. On Premium, $95.52 is due at checkout. After the term, the price is about 5.5 times higher. All About Cookies lists renewals as starting at $11.99 a month, while Hostinger's page shows $10.99. Check the price at checkout, and put the renewal price in your budget.

What is in the Black Friday offer?

The offer runs from 19 October to 14 December 2026. It lists a free domain for 1 year, free mailboxes for 1 year and double AI credits. You also get a 30-day money-back window if the plan does not fit.

How fast is Hostinger?

All About Cookies (updated 9 November 2026) tested a paid plan on a live test site. It ran speed tests from Montreal, Strasbourg and Dallas, monitored uptime for 9 days, and used Loader.io with 250 simultaneous visitors. It reported:

  • 100% uptime over the 9 days.
  • A page speed score of 99 out of 100 in all three locations.
  • A first contentful paint of 0.8 seconds and a largest contentful paint of 0.86 seconds.
  • An average response time of 138 ms, with a range of 133 to 253 ms.
  • An average of 14 ms under the 250-visitor load test.

Nine days is a short window, so treat the uptime figure as a snapshot.

What AI tools come with Hostinger?

Hostinger lists more AI tools than most low-cost hosts. You get an AI website builder, AI coding tools, AI email marketing and an AI assistant called Hostinger Agent. The plans come with a one-time bonus of 250 to 1,000 AI credits, depending on the plan. The credits are doubled in the Black Friday offer and expire when the hosting plan expires.

The Agent uses MCP and can run about 200 VPS management actions from chat. Hostinger also lists an MCP server for its API and a connector for tools such as Claude Code, Cursor and Codex. On custom domains it creates llms.txt, sitemap.xml and robots.txt files automatically.

Hostinger says that 85% of issues are solved without a human. Its blog says that 914,000 conversations were reviewed and 7% needed a person. Those are Hostinger's own claims. See our best AI web hosting guide for how other hosts compare.

Is Hostinger good for an online store?

All About Cookies notes that Hostinger is not PCI compliant. If you take card payments on the site, check how your payment provider handles that before you choose.
