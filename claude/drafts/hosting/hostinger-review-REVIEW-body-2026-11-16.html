Hostinger sells low-cost shared, cloud and VPS hosting, and it lists a long set of AI tools. This review is based on Hostinger's own pages and one named independent test. We did not test the host ourselves, and the sections below say which facts come from where. Facts were read on 15 and 16 November 2026.

What is Hostinger?

Hostinger is a low-price host with plans across shared, cloud and VPS hosting. Its Black Friday page runs from 19 October to 14 December 2026. The offer lists a free domain for 1 year, free mailboxes for 1 year and double AI credits. This review covers the three main plans in the pricing table below.

What do the sale and renewal prices mean?

The sale price needs a 48-month term. On Premium, $95.52 is due at checkout. The renewal price is about 5.5 times the sale price. All About Cookies lists renewals as starting at $11.99 a month, while Hostinger's page shows $10.99. Check the price at checkout.

How fast is Hostinger?

All About Cookies (updated 9 November 2026) tested a paid plan on a live test site. It ran speed tests from Montreal, Strasbourg and Dallas, monitored uptime for 9 days, and used Loader.io with 250 simultaneous visitors. It reported:

  • 100% uptime over the 9 days.
  • A page speed score of 99 out of 100 in all three locations.
  • A first contentful paint of 0.8 seconds and a largest contentful paint of 0.86 seconds.
  • An average response time of 138 ms, with a range of 133 to 253 ms.
  • An average of 14 ms under the 250-visitor load test.

Nine days is a short window, so treat the uptime figure as a snapshot.

Does Hostinger have AI features?

Hostinger lists more AI tools than most low-cost hosts. Its pages describe an AI website builder (once called Horizons), AI coding tools, AI email marketing and an AI assistant called Hostinger Agent. The plans come with a one-time bonus of 250 to 1,000 AI credits, depending on the plan. The credits are doubled in the Black Friday offer and expire when the hosting plan expires. The Agent uses MCP and can run about 200 VPS management actions from chat. Hostinger also lists an MCP server for its API and a connector for tools such as Claude Code, Cursor and Codex. On custom domains it creates llms.txt, sitemap.xml and robots.txt files automatically.

Some numbers come from Hostinger. It says that 85% of issues are solved without a human, and its blog says that 914,000 conversations were reviewed and 7% needed a person. Those are Hostinger claims. We did not test them. See our best AI web hosting guide for how other hosts compare.

Is Hostinger good for an online store?

All About Cookies notes that Hostinger is not PCI compliant. If you take card payments on the site, check how your payment provider handles that before you choose.
