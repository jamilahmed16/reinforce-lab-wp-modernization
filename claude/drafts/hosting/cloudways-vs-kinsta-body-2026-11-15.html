Cloudways and Kinsta both sell managed hosting for WordPress. They are built differently. Cloudways lets you pick the cloud provider and the server size and charges by the plan. Kinsta sells WordPress-only plans by monthly visits. This page compares what each host says on its own pages and what independent testers found, as of 15 November 2026. We did not test either host ourselves.

How do the prices compare?

The entry plans are not the same size, so compare with care. Cloudways' Flexible Micro plan on DigitalOcean lists at $14 a month. Kinsta's Launch plan lists at $35 a month and includes 75,000 visits and 15 GB of storage. Cloudways' Black Friday offer is 80% off for 2 months, and the page shows no end date. Kinsta's offer is 50% off for 3 months on monthly plans, or 3 months free on annual billing, and it ends on 1 December 2026.

What do the independent tests say?

  • Cloudways: DevOpsCube (updated 5 January 2026) tested WordPress on a 2 GB DigitalOcean server in New York. GTmetrix gave a grade of A with a 100% score, and edge page caching made pages 66% faster.
  • Kinsta: EXPERTE (updated 10 August 2026) tested a plan in the $20 range for 6 weeks. It reported 99.977% uptime, a time to first byte of 0.23 seconds and a largest contentful paint of 0.68 seconds.

The tests used different servers, plans and methods, so the numbers are not a race.

Which has AI features?

Cloudways lists Cloudways Copilot, an AI assistant that suggests fixes, and AI Agents plans with an MCP integration. Kinsta lists no built-in AI assistant on its pricing page. It has an API and publishes tutorials on building your own MCP server. We did not test any of these.

Who should pick which?

Pick Cloudways if you want to choose your cloud provider, start small and pay as you go. Pick Kinsta if you want WordPress-only hosting with human support and you can pay for it. If you are not sure, use Cloudways' free trial first.
