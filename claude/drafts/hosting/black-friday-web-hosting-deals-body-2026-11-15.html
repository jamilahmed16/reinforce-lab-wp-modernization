Ask three questions for any offer. How long must I commit to get this price? What is the price after that? Can I get my money back, and for how long? The picks above show those answers where the host's page gives them.

Eight checks before you pay

  1. Read the term. A 48-month price needs a 48-month payment, often up front.
  2. Read the renewal price. Put it in your calendar.
  3. Check who qualifies. Kinsta and ScalaHosting limit offers to new customers or new plans.
  4. Check the end date. Cloudways does not show one on the pricing page we read.
  5. Check the freebies. A free domain or free SSL certificate for the first year is not free for life. Ask what it costs in year two.
  6. Check the money-back guarantee. The hosts here list a 30-day money-back guarantee, except ScalaHosting, which claims an unconditional one. Read what it covers.
  7. Check migration. hosting.com lists free migration. Ask the others.
  8. Test before you move a live site. Copy the site, test it, check that support answers, then switch.
