Kinsta is premium managed WordPress hosting. It is fast, and Kinsta runs the server, the security and the support for you. It is also one of the most expensive hosts. The price does not jump at renewal, but you pay more for each site than on most budget hosts. This page puts the prices, the limits, the test results and the setup steps in one place. Prices and offers were read on Kinsta's own pages on 20 November 2026.

Is Kinsta worth it? The short version

  • Premium price, no renewal jump. The entry plan is $35 a month for one WordPress site and renews at $35.
  • Fast and steady in tests. EXPERTE measured a 0.23 s first response and the fewest slow responses of 14 hosts.
  • Support is chat only. Kinsta lists 24/7 human support in 8 languages and no phone line.
  • The first month is free until 1 December 2026 for new customers on select plans, and a 30-day money-back guarantee starts after the free period.
  • No email and no domain. You buy those elsewhere.

Why is Kinsta so expensive?

Kinsta's price pays for management, support and included tools, not raw hardware. WPThinker compares the $35 Single plan (1 site, 10 GB) with a $14 Cloudways server (25 GB, room for many sites). EXPERTE ranked Kinsta's entry plan the second most expensive of the 14 hosts it compared. Overage fees add to the cost if a site grows. Kinsta lists $0.50 per 1,000 extra visits, $0.50 per extra GB of server bandwidth, $0.05 per extra GB of CDN bandwidth and $2 per extra GB of disk a month, according to WPThinker. Kinsta warns you at 80% and 100% of your limit.

Some add-ons cost more. WPThinker lists Redis at $100 a site a month, backups every 6 hours at $20 a site, and hourly backups at $100. CuratorBits lists Kinsta Automatic Updates at $3 per environment a month.

What do you get with Kinsta?

  • Fully managed hosting. You do not patch PHP or set up caching. You use the MyKinsta dashboard.
  • Cloudflare security and a large CDN. WPThinker says every plan has Cloudflare's enterprise-level security, and a CDN with more than 300 edge locations.
  • 30 data centers. You choose one for each site.
  • Daily backups, kept for 14 to 30 days, and a free staging site.
  • Free, unlimited migrations, done by Kinsta's engineers, and free malware removal.
  • SSH and WP-CLI on every plan, according to CuratorBits.

Kinsta lists a 99.9% uptime guarantee, with 99.99% as a paid upgrade. It does not include email hosting or a domain.

Is Kinsta good for WordPress?

Yes, for business sites, busy blogs and stores that want WordPress managed for them. It does one thing, managed WordPress, and the platform is built around it. How is Kinsta support?

  • Channels: 24/7 human support in 8 languages, by chat. There is no phone line.
  • Speed: WP Umbrella says replies were under 5 minutes in its ticket history. CuratorBits says Kinsta puts its average first response under 2 minutes.
  • What customers say: Support is the feature reviewers praise most. The gap is the phone: if you want to call someone, Kinsta is not for you.

How do you build a WordPress site on Kinsta? Step by step

  1. Sign up and pick a plan. Choose Single 35k for a small site, or Single 65k or larger for a store. Use the first month free if you are a new customer.
  2. Choose a data center. Pick the one closest to your visitors. You choose one for each site.
  3. Move your site, or start a new one. Kinsta's engineers migrate an existing site for free. Or create a new WordPress install in MyKinsta.
  4. Connect your domain. Kinsta does not sell domains, so connect one you bought elsewhere.
  5. Test on staging. Use the free staging site to test updates before they go live.
  6. Check backups. Daily backups are on. Add more frequent backups only if you need them, because they cost extra.
  7. Watch your limits. Kinsta warns you at 80% and 100% of your visits. Set a reminder to review the plan.

Which Kinsta plan should you pick?

  • A personal blog or small business site: Single 35k.
  • A WooCommerce store or membership site: Single 65k or larger.
  • Two or more sites: WP 2 or WP 5.
  • An agency: Agency or Enterprise plans.

Final verdict: is Kinsta worth it in 2026?

Yes, if your WordPress site earns money or runs a business and you want it managed, fast and steady. It is not worth the price for a hobby site. Use the first month free before 1 December, and the 30-day guarantee after it, to test it.
