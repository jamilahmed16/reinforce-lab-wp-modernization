What did the independent tests find?

  • Hostinger: All About Cookies (updated 9 November 2026) reported 100% uptime over 9 days, a 99 out of 100 speed score and a 138 ms average response.
  • Bluehost: Gizmodo reported that the 99.9% uptime promise held in its test, with one outage of about a dozen minutes. 01net reported a fully loaded time of 1.9 seconds.

Bluehost also says its sites load in 0.35 seconds on average in the US, citing a WPShout test. Gizmodo notes that this is a result Bluehost presents, and that its own test showed a more modest picture. The tests differ in method, so they are not a direct race.

What about AI features?

Hostinger lists an AI website builder, AI credits, an AI assistant called Hostinger Agent and an MCP server. Bluehost lists AI site creation tools on all plans and AI-powered malware detection on its higher plans. We did not test any of them.

Which should you pick?

Pick Hostinger if you want the lowest sale price and the longest AI list, and can commit for 4 years. Pick Bluehost if you want 10 sites on the entry plan, a lower renewal price and phone support on the Business plan. Bluehost's Starter plan has chat support only.
