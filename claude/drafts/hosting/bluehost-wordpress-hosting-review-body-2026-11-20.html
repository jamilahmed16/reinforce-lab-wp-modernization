Bluehost is one of the best-known WordPress hosts, and WordPress.org lists it as a recommended host. It is easy for a first site, but the price depends on the term you pick, and the renewal is higher. This page puts the prices, the limits on each plan, the test results and the setup steps in one place. Prices were read on Bluehost's own pages on 20 November 2026.

Is Bluehost worth it? The short version

  • The price depends on the term. On the pricing page, Starter was $3.99 a month on a 36-month term and renews at $11.49. On the 12-month Black Friday offer it was $4.24 and renews at $13.79.
  • Easy for beginners. TechRadar praises the guided setup, the AI-assisted builder and the clear account panel.
  • Speed is average. In WPBeginner's July 2026 tests, Bluehost loaded in 933 ms against 659 ms for Hostinger.
  • Phone support starts on Business. Starter has chat only.
  • You can try it. Bluehost lists a 30-day money-back guarantee, but it deducts the domain fee if you cancel.

What are the hidden costs? Email, domain and refunds

  • Email: Professional Email starts as a one-month free trial. It then renews at $2.99 a month, according to Bluehost's pricing page. In an October 2026 cart check, Technoa5bar saw the trial added automatically.
  • Domain: The domain is free for the first year. After that it renews at the then current rate.
  • Refunds: You can cancel within 30 days for a refund of the hosting. The guarantee does not cover most add-ons, such as domains. If your plan included a free domain, Bluehost deducts the domain fee from your refund.
  • Taxes: The prices exclude VAT and other taxes.

Is Bluehost good for WordPress?

Yes, especially for a first site. Bluehost says it is recommended by WordPress.org. TechRadar's October 2026 review calls it one of the easiest recommendations for a first WordPress site. Why does Bluehost have negative reviews?

Search for Bluehost reviews and you will find Reddit threads with titles such as "why are Bluehost reviews so bad". The same three complaints come up in the reviews we read. First, the renewal price is much higher than the first-term price. Second, checkout adds extras such as email and domain add-ons. Third, speed trails LiteSpeed hosts. None of these is a hidden fact. They are why you should read the cart and the renewal price before you pay.

Bluehost pros and cons

Pros

  • A guided setup that suits a first WordPress site.
  • Recommended by WordPress.org, according to Bluehost.
  • Staging, SSH, WP-CLI, caching and a web application firewall on every plan.
  • A 99.99% uptime guarantee and a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Cons

  • The lowest prices need a 36-month term paid up front, and the renewal is higher.

How do you build a WordPress site on Bluehost? Step by step

  1. Choose a plan and a term. Starter covers 10 sites. Pick Business if you want phone support. Check the renewal price before you pay.
  2. Pick your domain. Choose a new domain, which is free for the first year, or use one you already own.
  3. Create your account. Untick any extras you do not want at checkout, such as email or add-ons.
  4. Follow the guided setup. Bluehost walks you through installing WordPress. Pick the onboarding level that fits you, because a wrong choice can mean starting again.
  5. Choose a theme and add pages. Add a home page, an about page and a contact page first. WonderBlocks gives you ready-made sections.
  6. Use staging for changes. The WordPress staging site is listed on every plan. Test updates there before they go live.
  7. Set up backups and a renewal reminder. Backups are weekly. Add the renewal date and the new price to your calendar.

Which Bluehost plan should you pick?

  • One blog or a small site: Starter.
  • Several sites, or you want phone support: Business.
  • A WooCommerce store: eCommerce Essentials, which adds the selling tools.
  • High traffic or full control: Bluehost's VPS and cloud plans. TechRadar calls the self-managed VPS range very competitive.

Final verdict: is Bluehost worth it in 2026?

Yes, if you are building a first WordPress site and want guidance more than the lowest long-term price. Pick the term carefully, read the renewal price, and use the 30-day window to test it. Skip it if speed is your top priority, or if your visitors are mostly outside the United States.
