Kinsta is a managed WordPress host aimed at businesses and agencies. It is one of the higher-priced hosts, and testers praise its speed and support. This review uses Kinsta's own pages and one named independent test. We did not test the host ourselves. Facts were read on 15 November 2026.

What is Kinsta?

Kinsta runs WordPress hosting only. Its plans are sold by monthly visits and storage, and it adds an agency tier and an enterprise tier. It lists a 30-day money-back guarantee, a 99.9% uptime guarantee and 24/7 human support in 8 languages.

How fast is Kinsta?

EXPERTE (updated 10 August 2026) tested a plan in the $20 range for 6 weeks, using the same Elementor WordPress site as other hosts. It reported:

  • 99.977% uptime.
  • A time to first byte of 0.23 seconds, with 0.09% of requests as outliers.
  • A largest contentful paint of 0.68 seconds.

EXPERTE rated support as AI and live chat, with no phone line. It lists the high cost as the main con.

Does Kinsta have AI features?

We found no built-in AI assistant on Kinsta's pricing page. Kinsta has a REST API, an APM tool for performance monitoring, and published tutorials on building your own MCP server for WordPress. Those tutorials are guides, not a Kinsta product. If you want AI tools bundled with your hosting, other hosts list more of them.

What are the pros and cons?

Pros:

  • Fast results and high uptime in the EXPERTE test.
  • 24/7 human support in 8 languages is listed.
  • A 30-day money-back guarantee is listed.
  • A performance monitoring tool and an API are available.

Cons:

  • High price compared with the other hosts on our Black Friday page.
  • The sale is for new customers only.
  • No phone support, according to EXPERTE.
  • No AI assistant on the pricing page.

Who should pick Kinsta?

Kinsta suits a business site where speed and support matter more than price. It is a weaker choice for a small blog on a tight budget. If you run many client sites, look at the agency tiers.
