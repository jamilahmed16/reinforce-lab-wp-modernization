Liquid Web is a US host known for managed WordPress, VPS and dedicated servers. It has also taken in the Nexcess brand. This review uses Liquid Web's own pages and Website Planet. We did not test the host ourselves. Facts were read on 15 and 16 November 2026.

What is Liquid Web by Nexcess?

TechRadar (updated 7 October 2025) said Nexcess is now fully absorbed into Liquid Web and that customers need to go to Liquid Web. Liquid Web's managed WordPress page now carries the Nexcess name. Website Planet's Nexcess review has been retitled as a Liquid Web review. If you searched for Nexcess, you are in the right place.

How fast is Liquid Web?

Website Planet (updated 23 March 2026) tested the entry Spark plan for one week. It saw no downtime. Pages loaded in 1 to 1.5 seconds at peak and 0.5 to 1 second when quiet. Live chat connected at once, and a ticket was answered in 15 minutes. One week is a short test, so treat it as a snapshot. Reader comments on that page range from praise for support to complaints about support after the takeover.

Does Liquid Web have AI features?

The managed WordPress page does not mention AI features. A reader review on the page praises "real people and not AI" for support. We found no AI tools to report.

What are the pros and cons?

Pros:

  • Managed WordPress with staging, backups and a firewall on every plan.
  • Object Cache Pro listed on the WordPress plans.
  • Human-assisted migration and 24/7/365 support are listed.
  • A path from WordPress to VPS and dedicated servers.

Cons:

  • Each plan covers 1 site at the entry price.
  • Website Planet said there is no free domain and no DDoS protection.
  • The page we read showed no money-back guarantee.
  • Prices rise quickly with traffic tiers.

Who should pick Liquid Web?

Liquid Web suits stores and busy sites that want managed care and room to grow. It costs more than shared hosting. If you want one cheap blog, look at Hostinger. For a managed cloud option, read our Cloudways review.
