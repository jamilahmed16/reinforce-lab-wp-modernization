Shared, VPS or dedicated hosting: which do you need?

Finding the Universe, which has hosted three sites on Cloudways since 2019, gives a simple guide. Shared hosting suits sites under about 10,000 visitors a month. A VPS suits sites above that, up to well over a million visitors depending on the design. Dedicated servers suit very large sites. Most of the hosts above sell shared plans first and let you move up later on the same account.

What should you check before you buy hosting?

  • The renewal price and date. Write both down before you pay.
  • The term. The lowest price often needs a 12, 36 or 48-month term paid up front.
  • The money-back window. It ranges from 30 days to 90 days. Use it to test speed and support.
  • Support channels. Phone support is rare. Network Solutions and InMotion list it. Hostinger and Kinsta do not.
  • Where your visitors are. Several hosts are fast near the US and slower in Asia without a CDN.
  • Card payments. Hostinger is not PCI compliant. If you take cards on your own site, check how your payment provider handles it.
