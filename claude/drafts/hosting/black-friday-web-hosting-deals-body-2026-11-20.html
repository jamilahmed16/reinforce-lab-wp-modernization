Is Black Friday a good time to buy web hosting?

It can be, if the deal lowers a price you would pay anyway. Prices move all year, and several hosts show a sale price on other dates too. Black Friday discounts apply to the first term only. Bluehost renews at the regular rate, Hostinger renews at $10.99, and hosting.com says its discounts apply to the first billing term only. Buy because the plan fits your site, then check the price you will pay in year two.

Can existing customers get Black Friday hosting deals?

It depends on the host. Kinsta's first month free and Cloudways' code are for new customers. Hostinger says you can claim its deal when you upgrade from hPanel. Bluehost's Buy More Save More applies to items in the same order. Ask the host's chat before you buy if you already have an account.

Eight checks before you pay

  1. Read the term. A 48-month price needs a 48-month payment, often up front.
  2. Read the renewal price. Put it in your calendar.
  3. Check who qualifies. Kinsta and Cloudways limit offers to new customers or new accounts, and ScalaHosting to new plans.
  4. Check the end date. Some pages disagree, as hosting.com's do.
  5. Check the freebies. A free domain or free email for the first year is not free for life. Ask what it costs in year two.
  6. Check the money-back guarantee. The hosts here list 30 days. Read what it covers: Bluehost, for example, deducts the domain fee from a refund.
  7. Check migration. Hostinger, hosting.com, Kinsta and Nexcess list free or assisted migration.
  8. Test before you move a live site. Copy the site, test it, check that support answers, then switch.
