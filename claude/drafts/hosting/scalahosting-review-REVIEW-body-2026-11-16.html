What did we find? The short version

  • Servers are steady under load. Two testers measured a 29 ms P95 response with 50 and 100 virtual users, and no failed requests in the second test.
  • Uptime is strong. Four independent tests recorded 100% uptime over 14 to 30 days.
  • The first page load is not the fastest. WPBeginner measured an 980 ms Largest Contentful Paint, slower than its 659 ms median for other hosts.
  • Renewals jump. Entry Cloud renews at $39.95 a month, about 2.7 times the $14.95 entry price.
  • There is no AI builder. ScalaHosting lists SShield, which it calls AI-powered security, and nothing more.

What is ScalaHosting, and who is behind it?

TechRadar (updated 16 October 2026) describes ScalaHosting as a Dallas-based host with 18 years in business. It sells shared hosting, managed cloud VPS plans and unmanaged cloud servers. The company builds its own control panel, SPanel, as an alternative to cPanel. It also lists SShield, a security tool that blocks brute-force attacks and bad IP addresses. ScalaHosting's own data centers are in Dallas, New York and Sofia. DigitalPrahlad counts 23 locations in total, with 20 coming from AWS.

What is the ScalaHosting uptime guarantee?

DigitalPrahlad points out that ScalaHosting states two different numbers. The standard shared hosting SLA is 99.9%, which allows about 43 minutes of downtime a month. The managed cloud and VPS pages state 99.99%. If ScalaHosting misses the SLA, the stated remedy is a service credit, not a cash refund. ScalaHosting also lists an anytime money-back guarantee on its own pages. Check the current terms for what it covers before you rely on either. In practice, WPBeginner, SimonHosting and Cybernews all recorded 100% uptime in their windows.

Is SPanel worth it? What is the control panel like?

SPanel is ScalaHosting's own control panel. SimonHosting measured an SPanel server with OpenLiteSpeed idling at about 435 MB of memory, against 1,840 MB for a cPanel baseline on a separate server. It treats that comparison as indicative, not lab grade. The same test showed 118 ms global TTFB on SPanel against 412 ms on the baseline. HostingAdvice notes one drawback: you work in two places, your account area and SPanel. If you are used to cPanel, the layout takes a short time to learn.

What support does ScalaHosting offer? Is there phone support?

WPBeginner lists 24/7 live chat, tickets and email. We did not find phone support listed. HostingAdvice reported that a real person answered and a ticket was fixed in 15 to 20 minutes. DigitalPrahlad reports live chat replies in 15 to 30 seconds, per ScalaHosting's own figures. If phone support matters to you, confirm it with ScalaHosting before you buy.

Who should choose ScalaHosting, and who should avoid it?

Choose it if your site has outgrown shared hosting and you want steady speed under load and a managed cloud server without full server work.

Avoid it if you want AI tools built in, the lowest price for years or phone support.
