InMotion Hosting is a US host founded in 2001. It sells shared, WordPress, VPS and dedicated plans, and it gives you 90 days to get your money back on shared and WordPress plans. This InMotion Hosting review reads InMotion's own homepage, six independent reviews with test numbers and the top-ranking pages. The pattern is clear. Shared plans are fast for small sites, the best price needs a long term, and the renewal price is several times the sale price. Here are the details.

What did we find? The short version

  • Shared hosting is fast for small sites. Webhostingcat measured a 100% GTmetrix score and a 135 ms time to first byte. SimonHosting measured 74 ms on a US East server.
  • Load handling is good. Webhostingcat reported no errors at 250, 500 and 1,000 concurrent clients, with a 433 ms average at 1,000.
  • The guarantee is long. TechRadar and Tom's Hardware report a 90-day money-back guarantee on shared plans.
  • Renewals are high. SimonHosting lists the Core plan at $2.29 a month, renewing at $10.00, about 4.4 times higher.
  • WooCommerce checkout is the weak spot. Webhostingcat saw a 775 ms time to first byte at checkout.

InMotion advertises a 99.9% uptime guarantee on shared plans, and some higher plans list 99.99%. SimonHosting measured 99.99% over 30 days. Does InMotion have a money-back guarantee? How long is it?

TechRadar and Tom's Hardware both report a 90-day money-back guarantee on shared and WordPress plans. That is three times the usual 30 days. Confirm it on the live page before you buy, because older reviews mention 45 days for some plans. A long window gives you time to move a site, test it and check support.

What is included with InMotion shared hosting?

  • Unlimited bandwidth on all shared plans.
  • A free domain for the first year and free SSL.
  • 10 email accounts on Core and unlimited email on higher plans, per SimonHosting.
  • UltraStack performance upgrades on Launch and above, with higher multipliers on higher plans.
  • Backups are not included on shared plans, according to TechRadar.
  • No staging on Core, according to SimonHosting.

Is InMotion good for WooCommerce and busy sites?

For small stores, yes. For heavy stores, look elsewhere. Webhostingcat says InMotion is ideal for light to moderate traffic and that heavy traffic or frequent spikes call for managed WordPress hosting or a cloud VPS. SimonHosting says performance under 50 or more simultaneous users on shared plans will show strain. If you expect a busy store, compare Cloudways and Kinsta.

How do you move a site to InMotion?

  1. Choose a plan and a data center close to your visitors. The reports we read measured US sites fastest.
  2. Ask InMotion about its migration help, or copy the site yourself with a migration plugin.
  3. Add a backup plugin, because shared plans do not include backups.
  4. Test pages, forms and checkout on the new server before you change DNS.
  5. Point your domain at InMotion, and watch rankings and errors for two weeks.
  6. Use the 90-day money-back window to check speed and support.
