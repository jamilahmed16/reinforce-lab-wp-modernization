InMotion Hosting is a US host founded in 2001. It sells shared, WordPress, VPS and dedicated plans. This review uses TechRadar and HostingAdvice, plus InMotion's own pages. We did not test the host ourselves. Facts were read on 15 and 16 November 2026.

What plans does InMotion sell?

TechRadar (updated 22 January 2026) lists four shared plans: Core, Launch, Power and Pro. Core is for one site. Launch allows 2 sites, Power 10 and Pro 40. InMotion also sells WordPress, VPS and dedicated servers.

How fast is InMotion?

TechRadar said that in its benchmark tests InMotion's shared plans held up better than most others it has seen. We did not find a recent public test with a full method and numbers. An older test from Top10 Website Hosting (March 2025) reported 100% uptime and a 553 ms average response on the Launch plan. That test is old, so treat it as a hint only.

What is it like to use?

HostingAdvice said InMotion makes setup easy for less experienced people. Its guides are clear, and there are many video tutorials. On VPS, HostingAdvice noted that cPanel costs extra and that it would have struggled without it. The chat widget on InMotion's site is an AI bot.

What are the pros and cons?

Pros:

  • A 90-day money-back guarantee on shared and WordPress plans, as reported by TechRadar.
  • A free domain, free SSL and email addresses on shared plans.
  • Shared, WordPress, VPS and dedicated plans in one account.
  • Clear setup guides.

Cons:

  • The best shared price needs a 3-year term.
  • No backups on shared plans.
  • cPanel costs extra on VPS.
  • Pricing is middle of the road after discounts, per HostingAdvice.

Who should pick InMotion?

InMotion suits people who want a long money-back window and room to grow from shared to VPS. It is a poor fit if you need the lowest price on a short term.
