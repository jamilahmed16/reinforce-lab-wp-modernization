Namecheap is best known for domains. It also sells shared hosting, VPS and a cheaper WordPress service. This review uses Namecheap's own pages and one named independent test. We did not test the host ourselves. Facts were read on 15 and 16 November 2026.

What does Namecheap hosting include?

Namecheap shared hosting has three plans: Stellar, Stellar Plus and Stellar Business. All three list unmetered bandwidth, free automatic SSL and a CDN for domains that use Namecheap's hosting nameservers. Data centers are in the US, the EU, Singapore and the UK.

How fast and reliable is Namecheap?

Cybernews (updated 15 May 2026) built a site and monitored it for two weeks. It measured 99.29% uptime and 94.20% reachability. Largest Contentful Paint was 1,135 ms from the US, 1.8 seconds from the UK and 2.7 seconds from India. A stress test with 50 visitors at once averaged 229 ms with no failed requests.

Namecheap's own page claims a 100% uptime guarantee. The Cybernews test measured less than that over two weeks. A guarantee and a measured result are different things, so read the terms before you count on it.

Does Namecheap have AI features?

Namecheap lists two AI tools on every shared plan. AI for WordPress starts from a simple prompt, builds a site, then helps you edit pages and fix common problems. Sitejet AI Website Builder builds a business site that you finish in a no-code editor. Namecheap's help pages say the WordPress AI works on new WordPress installs only. We did not test either tool.

What are the pros and cons?

Pros:

  • Low promo prices.
  • Three plans that all list unmetered bandwidth and free SSL.
  • Cybernews rated support very good.
  • Domains and hosting can sit in one account.

Cons listed by Cybernews:

  • Limited malware protection.
  • Builder templates look dated.
  • Renewals are more than double the promo price.
  • The measured uptime was below the 100% claim.

Who should pick Namecheap?

Namecheap suits a small first site on a tight budget. It is a weak fit if you need strong security tools or steady speed in Asia. Compare it with Hostinger vs Namecheap and Namecheap vs GoDaddy before you choose.
