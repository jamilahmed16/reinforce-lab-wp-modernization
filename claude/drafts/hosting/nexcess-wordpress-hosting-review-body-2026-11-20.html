Nexcess is managed WordPress and WooCommerce hosting, and it is now part of Liquid Web. Its managed plans start at $19 a month, and email hosting is included in every plan. This page covers the brand change, the prices, the limits, the test results and the setup steps. Prices were read on Nexcess's own plan page on 20 November 2026.

Is Nexcess worth it? The short version

  • The brand has changed. Nexcess's managed plans are also sold as "Liquid Web by Nexcess". The plans are the same product.
  • Plans from $19 a month. Pro is $19 and Elite is $29 for one site with up to 50,000 visits. Annual billing takes 10% off.
  • Email is included. Nexcess lists email hosting, staging, Object Cache Pro and an edge CDN on every plan.
  • Strong in load tests. Two testers saw 0% errors at 1,000 concurrent users.
  • Weak for Asia. There are no data centers in Asia, and first-byte times reached 1.9 seconds from India in one test.

Is Nexcess now Liquid Web?

Yes, in practice. Nexcess and Liquid Web announced in February 2024 that they would become one brand. TechRadar reported in October 2025 that the Nexcess brand had been absorbed into Liquid Web. In April 2026 the group relaunched Nexcess as its global brand and renamed the hosting arm "Liquid Web by Nexcess", according to HowToHosting. HostingEngines found that an old Nexcess WordPress page now returns an error, while the managed WordPress plans are sold on both nexcess.com and liquidweb.com. So a "Nexcess WordPress plan" and a "Liquid Web WordPress plan" are the same product. Check that the plan names and prices match before you pay.

How much does Nexcess cost?

Nexcess sells Pro and Elite managed WordPress plans. You choose how many sites and how many visits each site gets. These are the one-site prices for monthly billing, read on the Nexcess plan page on 20 November 2026 and checked against HostingEngines (5 November 2026).

The plan card loads its price with a script, so some readers see a blank price. The plan page lists "plans from $19 a month" for Pro and $29 for Elite. HostingEngines found that Pro and Elite get the same resources at each size, so the price gap is about features, not hardware. For one site with up to 50,000 visits, Pro has 20 GB of storage and Elite has 40 GB.

Extras: an extra site costs $9 a month on Pro and $12 on Elite. A visit pack of 100,000 more visits costs $10 a month. Nexcess says your first month over the visit limit is free, and after that extra visits cost $0.50 per 1,000. It warns you at 80% and 100% of your limit. Storage and visits are shared across the sites on a plan.

What does Nexcess cost after the first term?

The plan page shows one price and no renewal figure. That may mean the price stays the same, but the page does not say so. Real Tool Stack notes that the terms allow price increases with 30 days' notice, effective at your next renewal. Read the order summary before you pay, and note the renewal terms.

What resources are included with Nexcess hosting plans?

Every plan includes the tools above. The resources depend on the visit tier. For one site, 50,000 visits comes with 15 to 20 GB of storage and 2 TB of bandwidth on Pro. At 150,000 visits you get 40 GB and 3 TB, and at 400,000 you get 100 GB and 5 TB, according to HostingEngines. Daily backups are kept for 14 days on Pro and 30 days on Elite.

Does Nexcess offer WooCommerce and Magento hosting?

Who should use Nexcess, and who should look elsewhere?

Nexcess suits: business sites and WooCommerce stores with visitors in the US, UK or Australia, and people who want email, staging and migration included.

Look elsewhere if: most of your visitors are in Asia, you want a clear refund window and renewal price on the page, or you want the lowest price for a small site. Our Hostinger review and Bluehost review cover cheaper options.

How do you build a WordPress site on Nexcess? Step by step

  1. Pick sites and visits. Choose how many sites you need and the visits for each. Start low and move up when your numbers say so.
  2. Choose Pro or Elite. Elite adds more backups, more PHP workers and advanced malware protection.
  3. Check the cart. Note the plan name, the term and the renewal terms. Ask about the refund window.
  4. Move your site or start fresh. Nexcess offers human-assisted migration on every plan.
  5. Use staging. Every site gets a staging site and a development site. Test updates there.
  6. Set up email. Email hosting is included, so create your mailboxes.
  7. Watch your limits. Nexcess warns at 80% and 100% of your visits. Set a reminder to review the plan.

Final verdict: is Nexcess worth it in 2026?

Yes for business sites and stores that serve the US, UK or Australia and want email, staging and migration included. Check the renewal terms and refund window before you pay, because the plan page does not state them. Skip it if your visitors are mostly in Asia.
