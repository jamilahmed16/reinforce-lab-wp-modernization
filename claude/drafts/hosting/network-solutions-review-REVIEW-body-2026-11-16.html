Network Solutions is one of the oldest names in domains and hosting. It is easy to start with, and its prices are low on day one. Independent tests find it slower than most rivals, and the renewal price is about three times the promo price. This Network Solutions review reads Network Solutions' own plan page, five independent reports with real numbers and the top-ranking reviews. It puts the findings side by side, shows the real cost after year one and tells you who should buy elsewhere.

What did we find? The short version

  • It is easy to start. HostingAdvice found sign-up smooth and the AI website builder a standout.
  • Speed is below average. Tests measured a time to first byte of 402 to 900 ms and Largest Contentful Paint from 2.0 to 2.3 seconds on shared plans.
  • Uptime is acceptable. HostWise measured 99.89% over 34 weeks, close to the 99.9% guarantee.
  • Renewal is about three times the promo price. Starter goes from $2.99 to $8.83 a month.
  • Upselling is heavy. Reviewers describe checkout add-ons, auto-renewal by default and retention offers when you cancel.

What is Network Solutions, and how is it related to Domain.com?

Network Solutions is a registrar that also hosts. HostingDive says it feels like a legacy registrar add-on, not a purpose-built modern host. Domain.com is a related service: HowToHosting reports that parent company Newfold Digital announced in February 2026 that Domain.com is becoming Network Solutions, and that new hosting purchases now route to Network Solutions. TechRadar describes Domain.com as the same service Network Solutions offers. Our Domain.com review covers the legacy plans.

How steep is the Network Solutions renewal price?

Steep, and it applies to extras too. HostingAdvice praised Network Solutions for being transparent about renewal costs, which is fair. Its renewal prices are still among the highest. If you plan to stay, price the renewal and the extras together: domain, SSL and email. HostingDive also warns that purchases go on auto-renewal by default, so set a calendar reminder before the renewal date.

Is Network Solutions reliable? What is the uptime guarantee?

Network Solutions lists a 99.9% uptime guarantee for Linux plans, which allows about 8.75 hours of downtime a year. HostDean notes that the service agreement excludes downtime from Internet frailties, scheduled maintenance and causes outside its control, so the effective guarantee is narrower than the headline. HostWise measured 99.89%, and HostDean saw no major outages. Cloud-based hosting with failover helps stability.

What is Network Solutions support like? Is there phone support?

Yes, there is phone support. HostWise lists 24/7 phone and chat. HostingAdvice rated support B+ and got a human reply in about a minute after an AI introduction. HostingDive reports mixed quality, with complaints about wait times and slow escalation, and HostWise notes that support can take long at peak US business hours. If phone support matters to you, this is a point in favour. See our Hostinger review for a host with chat and email only.

Does Network Solutions have AI features?

Is Network Solutions good for WordPress?

Not the best. HostingAdvice's WordPress plan scored grade C and took 2.6 seconds to load a simple page with one post and one image. It called that fine for a small personal blog and not for a site that needs speed. If WordPress performance matters, compare Cloudways, Kinsta or Hostinger.

How do you move a site to Network Solutions?

  1. Choose a plan and decline add-ons you do not need.
  2. Copy your site with a migration plugin, or ask support for help.
  3. Test pages, forms and checkout on the new server before you change DNS.
  4. Point your domain at Network Solutions, and keep the old host for a few days.
  5. Turn off auto-renewal or set a reminder before the renewal date.

What mistakes should you avoid with Network Solutions?

  • Budgeting for $2.99 a month forever. Starter renews at $8.83.
  • Ignoring add-ons in the cart, such as SSL, email and security tools.
  • Leaving auto-renewal on without a reminder.
  • Choosing the cheapest plan for WordPress. HostingAdvice scored the WordPress plan a C.
  • Expecting a long money-back window. We did not find one on the hosting page.

What are the disadvantages of Network Solutions?

  • Renewal is about three times the promo price.
  • Speed is below average, with no CDN or NVMe storage.
  • Heavy upselling at checkout and auto-renewal by default.
  • No free SSL or free domain on the cheapest plan.
  • Support quality is inconsistent.
  • Domain and SSL renewals are expensive.

Who should choose Network Solutions, and who should avoid it?

Choose it if you want an easy start, phone support and one company for your domain and site, and you will read the checkout carefully.

Avoid it if you want fast pages, a low price for years or a simple checkout. HostWise only recommends it when a legacy portfolio already lives there.
