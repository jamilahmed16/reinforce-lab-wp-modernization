Spaceship is a newer domain and hosting company with some of the lowest shared hosting prices around. It gives you a 30-day free trial, a site builder with AI tools and security software at no extra cost. Independent tests found fast US speeds and strong uptime. The catches are extras that cost more, no phone support and renewals that are higher than the first year. This Spaceship hosting review reads Spaceship's own pages, five independent reports and the top-ranking reviews, and it explains the promo codes on its sale page.

What did we find? The short version

  • Prices are very low. Essential is $19.88 for the first year, which is $1.66 a month, after a 30-day free trial.
  • Speed is good near the US. SimonHosting measured a 242 ms time to first byte and a 1.4 second Largest Contentful Paint on a WooCommerce test build.
  • Uptime is strong. SimonHosting recorded 99.992% over 90 days. Scribeage recorded 100% over 30 days.
  • Check the extras. Email is free (5 mailboxes per domain on Essential, 25 for a year on Pro and Supreme), but TechRadar says backups start at $11.76 for 5 GB on a two-year plan.
  • No phone support. SimonHosting lists this as a caveat.
