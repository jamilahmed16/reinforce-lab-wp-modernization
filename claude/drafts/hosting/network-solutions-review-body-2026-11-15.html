Network Solutions is one of the oldest names in domains and hosting. This review uses Network Solutions' own pages and a three-week test from HostingAdvice. We did not test the host ourselves. Facts were read on 15 and 16 November 2026.

What does Network Solutions hosting include?

There are three shared plans on a one-year term. All three list a drag and drop builder and expert support.

How fast is Network Solutions?

HostingAdvice tested three plans for three weeks with GTmetrix. The cheap plan scored grade B with 86%, and its Largest Contentful Paint reached 2.0 seconds. The WordPress plan scored grade C with 2.6 seconds. The eCommerce plan scored grade A with 1.2 seconds. HostingAdvice said the host has no CDN and no NVMe storage, and it called performance a bit below average. It rated support B+, with a human reply in about a minute after an AI introduction.

Does Network Solutions have AI features?

HostingAdvice praised the AI website builder. Our read of the hosting page found no AI tools listed. Network Solutions sells an AI All-Access Pack in its deals blog. We did not test it.

What are the pros and cons?

Pros:

  • An easy sign-up that gets a beginner online quickly.
  • An AI website builder that HostingAdvice liked.
  • Clear renewal prices, as HostingAdvice noted.
  • Support that answered fast in the test.

Cons:

  • Slower than rivals in the test.
  • No CDN or NVMe storage.
  • No free SSL or free domain on the cheapest plan, per HostingAdvice.
  • Renewal prices are about three times the promo price on the entry plan.

Who should pick Network Solutions?

It suits a beginner who wants an easy start and phone or chat help. If speed matters, compare Hostinger or Cloudways. Our Domain.com review covers Domain.com, whose support appears under the Network Solutions brand.
