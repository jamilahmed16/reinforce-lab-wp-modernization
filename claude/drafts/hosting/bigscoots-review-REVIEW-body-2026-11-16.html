BigScoots is an independent managed WordPress host based in Chicago, founded in 2010. It sells managed WordPress from $35 a month, cheaper shared plans and managed cloud servers. Its pitch is people: free migrations, monitoring every 60 seconds and human support around the clock. This BigScoots review reads BigScoots' own pricing page, five independent sources and the top-ranking reviews. One of them measured thousands of live BigScoots sites, which is a useful test for a host that few people benchmark. Here are the results, the prices and the fine print on the uptime guarantee.

What did we find? The short version

  • Real-world speed is strong. A crawl of 5,779 BigScoots sites in October 2026 found a 153 ms median time to first byte, fourth of 13 managed WordPress hosts, against a typical 342 ms.
  • Core Web Vitals are good. 82% of WordPress sites on BigScoots pass, against a typical 59%, according to the same crawl.
  • Prices are clear. Essential 75 is $35 a month, or $350 a year, with no renewal jump listed.
  • The uptime promise has fine print. Marketing pages say 99.99%, while the formal agreement says 99.9%.
  • No Black Friday sale. BigScoots says on its own blog that it runs none.

What is BigScoots?

HostProdigy lists BigScoots as founded in 2010 and headquartered in Chicago, with data centers in Chicago and Ashburn, Virginia. It owns its hardware in Chicago. It sells shared hosting, managed WordPress, managed cloud VPS and dedicated servers. Managed WordPress is the flagship. BigScoots is not part of a large hosting group. Hostalog notes that the small number of data centers may limit global reach.

What is the BigScoots uptime guarantee?

BigScoots' own pricing page lists a 99.99% uptime SLA on its managed WordPress plans. CompareBestAI adds that the published service level agreement, last updated in December 2022, defines the formal guarantee as 99.9% per monthly billing period, so read the agreement attached to your plan. Hostalog describes the remedy: no credit for the first 59 minutes, then 5% of your monthly fee for each further full hour. Scheduled maintenance of up to 24 hours a year is excluded. For a blog the difference does not matter. For a store or membership site, confirm which SLA applies to your plan before you buy.

What is BigScoots support like?

BigScoots lists 24/7/365 human support with an average reply of about 90 seconds. That is the host's own claim, and the independent reports we read mostly describe support in the same terms: hands-on and proactive. Sites are monitored every 60 seconds, and an alert goes to the support team if a performance or security issue appears. We found no independent timed test of support. HostProdigy lists tickets and 24/7 chat. If a person on the other end matters, test support in your first weeks inside the 45-day window.

How do you move a site to BigScoots?

  1. Choose a plan that matches your monthly visits and storage.

What are the disadvantages of BigScoots?

  • It costs more than shared hosting, from $35 a month.
  • No recent lab test of a plan with a full method.
  • The formal uptime SLA is 99.9%, lower than the 99.99% on marketing pages.
  • Few data centers, in Chicago and Ashburn.
  • No Black Friday sale and no AI tools.
  • Extra installs and manual backups cost extra.

Who should choose BigScoots, and who should avoid it?

Choose it if you run a professional WordPress site, want human support and free migration, and value steady performance over the lowest price.

Avoid it if you run a hobby site on a tight budget, your visitors are mostly far from the US, or you want AI tools built in.
