"AI hosting" means three different things, and many guides mix them up. This page sorts hosts by what they list on their own pages, as of 16 November 2026. We did not test any of the AI features. We did not find an independent test of them either, so every feature below is the host's own claim.

What does AI web hosting mean?

  • AI tools with your hosting. A site builder, an assistant or credits that come with a normal hosting plan. This suits people who want to build a site faster.
  • Hosting to run AI agents and apps. A server sized for an agent, with room for the memory and bandwidth it needs. This suits people who run tools such as n8n or their own agents.
  • An AI-ready developer cloud. GPU servers, model services and APIs for people who build AI apps.

Pick the meaning first. A site builder will not help you run an agent, and a GPU server is far too much for a small business site.

How do the AI credits work at Hostinger?

Hostinger's AI page says every hosting plan includes a one-time bonus of 250 to 1,000 AI credits, depending on the plan. They do not renew each month. Credits that come with a plan expire when the plan expires, and top-up or bonus credits expire 3 months after they are added. The credits pay for building and editing sites and for AI features, and complex requests cost more than simple ones. Support requests to the Hostinger Agent are free, while business requests, such as strategy or outreach, use credits. The Black Friday offer lists double AI credits.

How do you choose?

  • You want to build a site faster: compare Hostinger, hosting.com and Namecheap on price and renewal.
  • You want to run an agent or n8n: look at an agent plan or a VPS with enough memory, and check where your model key goes.
  • You build AI apps: look at a developer cloud, and price the GPU hours before you commit.
  • You care about price after year one: read the renewal price. Our Black Friday deals page shows it for each host.
