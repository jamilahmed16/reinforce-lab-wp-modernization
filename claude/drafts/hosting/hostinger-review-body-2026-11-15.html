What is Hostinger?

Hostinger is a low-price host that runs a large set of plans across shared, cloud and VPS hosting. Its Black Friday page runs from 19 October to 14 December 2026. This review covers the main shared and cloud plans, and the AI tools that come with them.

How fast is Hostinger?

All About Cookies (updated 9 November 2026) tested a paid plan on a live test site. It ran speed tests from Montreal, Strasbourg and Dallas, monitored uptime for 9 days, and used Loader.io with 250 simultaneous visitors. It reported:

  • 100% uptime over the 9 days.
  • A page speed score of 99 out of 100 in all three locations.
  • A first contentful paint of 0.8 seconds and a largest contentful paint of 0.86 seconds.
  • An average response time of 138 ms, with a range of 133 to 253 ms.
  • An average of 14 ms under the 250-visitor load test.

Nine days is a short window, so treat the uptime figure as a snapshot.

Is Hostinger good?

On the numbers above, yes for speed. All About Cookies lists these cons: Hostinger is not PCI compliant, security is basic, and renewals are higher than sale prices. If you run an online store that takes card payments, check how your payment provider handles PCI before you choose.

Does Hostinger have AI features?

Hostinger lists more AI tools than most low-cost hosts. Its pages describe an AI website builder (once called Horizons), AI coding tools, AI credits of 500, 1,000 or 2,000 depending on the plan, AI email marketing, and an AI assistant called Hostinger Agent. The Agent uses MCP and can run about 200 VPS management actions from chat. Hostinger also lists an MCP server for its API and a connector for tools such as Claude Code, Cursor and Codex. On custom domains it creates llms.txt, sitemap.xml and robots.txt files automatically.

Some of the numbers come from Hostinger. It says that 85% of issues are solved without a human, and its blog says that 914,000 conversations were reviewed and 7% needed a person. Those are Hostinger claims. We did not test them.

What are the pros and cons?

Pros:

  • Very low sale prices.
  • A 30-day money-back guarantee is listed.
  • Fast results and 100% uptime in a short test.
  • A wide set of AI tools is listed.

Cons:

  • The sale price needs a 48-month term.
  • Renewals are about 5.5 times the sale price on Premium.
  • Not PCI compliant, and security is basic, according to All About Cookies.

Who should pick Hostinger?

Hostinger suits a new site owner who wants a low price and AI tools, and who can commit for four years. It is a weaker choice if you want a short term, or if you take card payments directly on the site. Compare it with hosting.com if a shorter term matters.
