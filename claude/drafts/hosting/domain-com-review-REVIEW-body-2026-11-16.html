Domain.com sells domains, a drag and drop website builder and shared hosting. The first-year hosting prices are low, the renewal prices are about three times higher, and we could not find a recent independent speed or uptime test with a stated method. This Domain.com hosting review reads Domain.com's own plan page, four independent reviews and the top-ranking pages. It is clear about what is known and what is not. It also shows the three-year cost, and it explains why Domain.com is now being moved onto Network Solutions.

What did we find? The short version

  • First-year prices are low. Starter is $35.88 for the first year, which is $2.99 a month.
  • Renewals are about three times higher. Starter renews at $105.99 a year, Essential at $184.99 and Premium at $224.99.
  • The uptime guarantee is weak. Domain.com lists 99%, which allows about 7 hours of downtime a month. Most hosts promise 99.9%.
  • No independent speed test. Two reviewers searched and found none with a stated method, so we make no speed claims.
  • Hosting is moving to Network Solutions. HowToHosting reports that Domain.com is becoming Network Solutions.

Is Domain.com the same as Network Solutions?

They are closely related. Both sit under Newfold Digital, the group that also owns Bluehost and HostGator, according to Technoa5bar. HowToHosting reports that Newfold announced in February 2026 that Domain.com is becoming Network Solutions. It says new hosting purchases now route to Network Solutions, which sets its own rates, and that the standalone Domain.com plans are legacy pricing that affiliate sites still quote. TechRadar (updated 2 July 2026) describes Domain.com as the same service Network Solutions offers. HostAdvice found that Domain.com support appears under the Network Solutions brand. Read our Network Solutions review for the plans you are most likely to buy.

How fast and reliable is Domain.com hosting?

We do not know, and the independent sources say the same. Technoa5bar searched and found no recent, documented independent test of Domain.com shared hosting with a stated method. HowToHosting found no independent benchmark data from major testing platforms. So we make no speed claims. What we have is the uptime promise: Domain.com lists a 99% uptime guarantee, which allows about 7 hours of downtime a month. HowToHosting lists 99.9% in its review, which differs from the page we read. User reviews are mixed on whether real uptime matches the promise, with some reports of DNS delays and email outages. Our advice: test any site on a trial with a free uptime monitor before you move it. For measured speed on a related service, see the Network Solutions test results.

What is Domain.com support like?

HostAdvice (8 May 2026) signed up, checked out and sent a technical pre-sales question. Chat opened with five bot prompts, then a human gave an accurate answer. Domain.com lists 24/7 chat. TechRadar notes phone support and heavy upselling. If support is a priority, test it early.

What does Domain.com include?

  • A drag and drop website builder on every plan. HostAdvice reports an AI website builder on all plans, but our read of the hosting page did not list one.
  • Free SSL, and a free domain for 1 year on Essential and Premium.
  • Domain privacy is on by default, according to HostAdvice, while TechRadar says free domain privacy is not included. Check the checkout.
  • Domain lock and 24/7 chat.
  • No VPS plans, according to HostAdvice.

Is Domain.com good for beginners?

It is easy to start. The builder is included and checkout is simple. The risks are the upselling TechRadar describes, the steep renewal and the move to Network Solutions, which makes the account area and support less clear. If you want a beginner host with published speed results, compare Hostinger or Namecheap.

Which Domain.com plan should you pick?

  • Starter: one small site with up to 15,000 visitors a month. It has no free domain or SSL, so add those costs.
  • Essential: up to 3 sites and 50,000 visitors, with a free domain and SSL for the first year. It is the plan with the most value in year one, and it renews at $184.99.
  • Premium: up to 10 sites and 200,000 visitors. Storage stays at 20 GB, so check that it fits.
  • Anything bigger: Domain.com has no VPS, so a growing site moves to another host.

What happens to Domain.com hosting during the move to Network Solutions?

We could not confirm the details. HowToHosting says new hosting purchases route to Network Solutions and that standalone Domain.com plans are legacy pricing. Technoa5bar read the Domain.com plan page as live on 12 October 2026. If you are an existing customer, ask support what your renewal price and plan name will be, and keep a copy of the answer. If you are a new buyer, price both Domain.com and Network Solutions at checkout, because the rates can differ.

How do you move a site to Domain.com?

  1. Choose a plan, and read the cart total for renewal and add-ons.
  2. Build with the drag and drop builder, or copy an existing site with a migration plugin.
  3. Test pages and forms, and set up a free uptime monitor.
  4. Point your domain at Domain.com, and keep the old host for a few days.
  5. Turn off auto-renewal, or set a reminder before the renewal date.

What are the disadvantages of Domain.com?

  • Renewal is about three times the first-year price.
  • A 99% uptime guarantee, lower than most hosts.
  • No independent speed or uptime test found.
  • Heavy upselling, according to TechRadar.
  • No VPS plans, and the product is moving to Network Solutions.

Who should choose Domain.com, and who should avoid it?

Choose it if you already buy domains there and want a very simple first site, and you will move after year one.

Avoid it if you plan to stay for years, you want proven speed or you want a strong uptime promise.
