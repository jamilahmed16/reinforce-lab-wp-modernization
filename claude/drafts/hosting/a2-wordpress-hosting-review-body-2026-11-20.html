What happened to A2 Hosting?

World Host Group bought A2 Hosting in January 2025. The company then renamed it hosting.com. Its own blog post says "A2 Hosting is now hosting.com". The old plan names changed too: Startup became Starter, Drive became Plus, Turbo Boost became Pro and Turbo Max became Max. If you are an A2 customer, your account and your plan now sit under hosting.com. The managed WordPress plans are built on Rocket.net, which joined the group in August 2025, according to HostDecider.

Is the A2 Hosting money-back guarantee still real?

Yes, but it is 30 days now. hosting.com's guarantee covers new purchases of shared, WordPress, email and reseller hosting. It does not cover domain registrations, renewals, VPS plans, dedicated servers, SSL certificates or third-party products. If you cancel, hosting.com refunds the hosting fees and takes off any domain fee. A2 Hosting used to advertise a longer anytime guarantee, so read the current terms fresh.

How do you cancel A2 Hosting?

Contact support within 30 days of buying if you want a refund. Outside that window, cancel before the renewal date, because plans renew automatically and a saved payment method may be charged three days before it.

How do you build a WordPress site on hosting.com? Step by step

  1. Choose shared or managed WordPress. Several sites and email: shared. One WordPress site managed for you: a WordPress plan.
  2. Pick a term and check the regular price. The renewal is the regular price, so write it down.
  3. Claim your free domain. It is free for the first year. Check what it costs to renew.
  4. Install or migrate WordPress. Use the one-click install, or ask for free managed migration if your site is elsewhere.
  5. Pick a theme and add pages. Add a home page, an about page and a contact page first.
  6. Use staging on Pro and above. Test updates before they go live.
  7. Set a renewal reminder. Cancel or change plans before the renewal date if the new price does not suit you.

Which hosting.com plan should you pick?

  • A blog or a few small sites: Starter.
  • More sites and staging: Pro.
  • Many sites and priority support: Max.
  • One WordPress site managed for you: WordPress Small, or Medium for up to five sites.

Final verdict: is A2 Hosting (hosting.com) worth it in 2026?

Yes for a low first-year bill, and no for a low price over many years. Starter's renewal is $13.99 a month, so plan to move or change plans after the first term. Use the 30-day refund to test it, and choose a different host if you want low prices for years.
