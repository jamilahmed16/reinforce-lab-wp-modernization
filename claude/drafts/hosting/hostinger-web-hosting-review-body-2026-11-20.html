Is Hostinger worth it? The short version

  • The price is real, and so is the renewal. Premium costs $1.99 a month on a 48-month term, then renews at $10.99 a month.
  • Speed and uptime test well. Five independent tests measured server response between 227 and 447 ms and uptime between 99.96% and 100%.

  • Support is chat and email. There is no phone line, according to two testers.
  • You can try it. Hostinger lists a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Why is Hostinger so cheap?

Hostinger says it keeps prices low with server optimization and smart pricing. In practice, the low price comes from the long prepaid term. You pay for four years at checkout, and Hostinger gets the cash up front. After that the price rises. That trade is fair if you know about it and plan to keep the site.

What do you get with each Hostinger plan?

  • Premium suits one small site or up to three. It has weekly backups, a CDN, free SSL and 2 mailboxes per site for a year.
  • Unlimited adds daily backups, unlimited websites and mailboxes, staging, object cache, on-demand backups and WordPress multisite. For most growing sites this is the better buy.
  • Cloud Startup adds a dedicated IP address, 4 CPU cores, 4 GB of RAM and 100 PHP workers. It fits agencies and sites with steady traffic.

Gizmodo came to the same view after buying Unlimited: the extra $1 a month over Premium paid for daily backups, a CDN and more AI credits. Its writers called Unlimited the better buy for most sites.

How do you build a WordPress site on Hostinger? Step by step

This is the short setup guide that many readers come here for. It follows Hostinger's own help pages.

  1. Choose a plan and pay. All plans are paid upfront. The monthly price is the total divided by the number of months. Pick Premium for one small site, or Unlimited if you want staging and daily backups.
  2. Claim your free domain. Log in to hPanel, Hostinger's control panel, and click Claim domain. You must register it within 12 months of buying the plan, or the offer is cancelled. Not every domain ending is eligible.
  3. Or point a domain you already own. If your domain is at another company, point its nameservers to Hostinger, or transfer it. Hostinger has a help page for each.
  4. Install WordPress. Use the one-click installer in hPanel. If you already have a WordPress site elsewhere, ask for the free migration instead. It works for WordPress and other open-source sites only.
  5. Check SSL. Hostinger sets up a free SSL certificate when you sign up. You do not need to activate it.
  6. Turn on caching. Make sure LiteSpeed Cache is active in WordPress. Gizmodo's second test, which turned on LiteSpeed Cache and the CDN, cut its response time almost in half.
  7. Pick a theme and add your pages. Add a home page, an about page and a contact page first. Then set up your SEO plugin and a backup routine.
  8. Set a renewal reminder. Put the renewal date and the new price in your calendar so the increase is never a surprise.

Hostinger also includes its AI website builder on every plan. You describe the site, and the builder makes a first version that you can edit. The builder runs on credits that come as a one-time gift, and the Black Friday offer doubles them.

How many websites can you host on Hostinger Premium?

Premium allows 3 websites. Unlimited and Cloud Startup allow unlimited websites, subject to Hostinger's Fair Usage Policy. Each plan also has limits on storage, PHP workers and files, called inodes. Premium allows 400,000 inodes, Unlimited 600,000 and Cloud Startup 2,000,000. A WordPress site with many images and plugins uses files faster than you may expect.

Is Hostinger good for an online store?

It can run one. Hostinger says every plan works with WordPress and WooCommerce. Its own Ecommerce store builder is not available on Premium. It is included on Unlimited and Cloud Startup, with unlimited products and no platform fee. For a busy WooCommerce store, pick Unlimited or Cloud Startup, because the extra RAM, CPU and PHP workers matter when many shoppers load pages at once.

All About Cookies notes that Hostinger is not PCI compliant. If you take card payments on your own site, use a payment provider that handles the card step.

How is Hostinger support and security?

  • Support: 24/7 live chat and email. There is no phone support, according to WPBeginner and HostingTestLab. Chat starts with an AI assistant and moves to a person when needed.
  • Priority support: WPBeginner and Gizmodo both say priority support starts on the Unlimited plan.
  • Security: Hostinger lists a web application firewall, a malware scanner, DDoS protection with Cloudflare-protected nameservers, free SSL and a secure access manager.
  • What customers say: Cybernews reports a Trustpilot score in the 4.4 to 4.7 range. Reddit threads are mixed. People like the price and complain about renewals and AI-led support.

How do you get the best price?

  • Choose the longest term only if you are sure you will keep the site for four years. The 30-day money-back guarantee gives you a safe first month.
  • Note the renewal date and compare prices a few months before it.
  • Claim the free domain in the first year, and check its renewal price.
  • Do not budget for $1.99 a month forever. Plan for about $3.79 a month over five years on Premium, or $10.99 at renewal.

Final verdict: is Hostinger worth it in 2026?

Yes, if you want a fast, low-cost home for a WordPress site and you plan for the renewal. Start on Premium for one small site, or Unlimited if you want staging and daily backups.
