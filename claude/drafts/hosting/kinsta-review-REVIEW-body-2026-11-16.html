What is Kinsta?

Kinsta is a managed WordPress host founded in 2013 and based in Los Angeles. It builds on Google Cloud Platform's premium network and runs 27 data centers on 6 continents. WP Umbrella describes the stack as Nginx, LXD containers and MariaDB, with Cloudflare Enterprise integration for edge caching and DDoS protection. Kinsta does one thing: managed WordPress and WooCommerce hosting. It does not sell domains, a site builder or email. WP Umbrella lists TripAdvisor and Calm among its notable customers.

Is Kinsta support great? What do customers say?

Support is the feature reviewers praise most. WP Umbrella says support is human, fast and competent, with replies in under 5 minutes in its own ticket history. Webhostingcat lists 24/7/365 expert support. EXPERTE notes that support runs through chat with AI help and has no phone line. The common view is that the team solves WordPress problems well. The gap is the phone: if you want to call someone, Kinsta is not for you.

What is included in Kinsta plans?

  • Free SSL with wildcard certificates, free migrations and free hack fixes.

We found no built-in AI assistant on Kinsta's pricing page. Kinsta has a REST API and publishes tutorials for building your own MCP server for WordPress. Those tutorials are guides, not a Kinsta product. If you want AI tools bundled with your hosting, see our best AI web hosting guide.

How secure is Kinsta, and what is the infrastructure?

Kinsta builds on Google Cloud Platform's premium network. WP Umbrella says every site runs in its own LXD container, with no shared IP pools and no noise from other tenants, which is a common problem on shared hosting. Security includes wildcard SSL certificates on every plan, hardware firewalls, DDoS detection at the network layer, two-factor authentication and the Cloudflare integration. Webhostingcat adds free hack fixes: if a site is hacked, Kinsta says it will clean it up. The database is MariaDB, and the stack supports current PHP versions.

How do backups, staging and the MyKinsta dashboard work?

Daily backups, staging environments and free SSL come with every plan, according to WP Umbrella. A staging environment is a private copy of your site where you test plugin and theme changes before you go live. You can restore a backup from the dashboard. How long backups are kept depends on the plan, so check that on Kinsta's page. WP Umbrella calls the MyKinsta dashboard really usable and not a 2012 cPanel descendant. Webhostingcat found it intuitive, with domains, backups, the CDN and analytics easy to find, and it shows a checklist when you add a site.

How do you move a site to Kinsta?

  1. Sign up, choose a data center close to your visitors and add your site.
  2. Ask Kinsta to migrate it. Migrations are free, and WP Umbrella says the team moved sites from OVH and O2Switch without help.
  3. Test the copy on a staging address: pages, forms, checkout and speed.
  4. Point your domain at Kinsta, and keep the old host for a few days.
  5. Watch rankings, errors and speed for two weeks.

Is Kinsta worth the money?

It depends on what a slow or broken site costs you. Kinsta's entry price is $35 a month. A shared host such as Hostinger starts near $2 a month on a sale and renews at $10.99 a month. The gap pays for daily backups, staging, a CDN, free migrations and a support team that fixes WordPress problems. WP Umbrella says Kinsta is not the right fit for small sites with little traffic, but for agencies, WooCommerce stores and high-traffic publishers it is one of the strongest managed hosts. A good rule: if a day of downtime costs you more than a year of the price gap, Kinsta is worth a look.

Is Kinsta good for WooCommerce?

Yes. WP Umbrella monitors a mix of content sites and WooCommerce stores on Kinsta. Webhostingcat's load test finished 1,000 requests with no timeouts. Check the visit limit of your plan, because overage costs extra, and plan for storage: WP Umbrella notes that single-site plans cap storage at 10 to 15 GB on most tiers.

What are the disadvantages of Kinsta?

  • The price floor of $35 a month is hard to justify for a small brochure site.
  • There is no phone support.
  • Visit limits and storage limits are strict on the lower plans.
  • It sells no email, domains or site builder.
  • No built-in AI features.

Is Kinsta the best managed WordPress hosting in 2026?

It is one of the best, and the answer depends on what you need. Kinsta wins on speed, support and included tools. Cloudways wins on price and control. WP Engine posted a faster response time in Hostingstep's test. Hostinger wins on cost for small sites. If you compare managed WordPress hosting providers, list your must-haves first: phone support, a low price, agency tools or WooCommerce speed.

Who should use Kinsta, and who should avoid it?

Use it if your site earns money and speed and support matter more than price. It also suits agencies that manage many client sites.

Avoid it if you run a small blog on a tight budget, want one account for domains and email, or want phone support.
