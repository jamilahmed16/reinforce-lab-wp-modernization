## C. Article 4 AI literacy — AMENDED (VERIFIED via Service Desk disclaimer + independent legal sources)
Digital Omnibus on AI = Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, published OJ 24 Jul 2026, in force 27 Jul 2026. Article 4 replaced:
providers and deployers "shall take measures to support the development of AI literacy"; it "does not require providers or
deployers to guarantee any specific level of AI literacy of any individual"; Commission and Member States support (esp. SMEs).
The Service Desk Article 4 page still shows the original text with an "amended — not yet updated" disclaimer.
