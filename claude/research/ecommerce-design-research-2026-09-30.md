## B. Verified facts
- Baymard (updated 22 Sep 2025): average documented cart abandonment 70.22% (50 studies). Excluding "just browsing" (42% of US
  shoppers): extra costs 40%, delivery too slow 20%, didn't trust site with card 19%, forced account 18%, long/complicated
  checkout 17%, site errors 17%, returns policy 13%, couldn't see total up front 12%, card declined 10%, not enough payment
  methods 9%. Ideal checkout 12–14 form elements vs average US 23.48. — https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate
- Google product structured data: product snippets vs merchant listings (pages where customers can buy); return policy and
  loyalty under Organization; data via markup or Merchant Center feeds. — https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product
- Google ecommerce best practices hub (URL structure, faceted navigation, pagination). — https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce
- European Commission: EAA covers e-commerce (applies from 28 June 2025). Caution: the EAA's harmonised standard (EN 301 549)
  maps to WCAG 2.1 AA — do not say the EAA "requires WCAG 2.2".
