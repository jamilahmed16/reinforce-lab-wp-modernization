## C. Google (primary, fetched)
- Three stages: crawling, indexing, serving; "Google doesn't accept payment to crawl a site more frequently, or rank it
  higher. If anyone tells you otherwise, they're wrong."; indexing isn't guaranteed. — /search/docs/fundamentals/how-search-works
- "No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google." Services an SEO provides: review of site content or structure; technical
  advice (hosting, redirects, error pages, JavaScript); content development; management of online business development
  campaigns; keyword research; SEO training; expertise in specific markets and geographies; optimizing for generative AI.
  "If you run a small local business, you can probably do much of the work yourself." — /search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo
- Starter Guide: changes take hours to several months; wait a few weeks to assess; good titles are unique, clear, concise,
  accurate; duplicate content "is not a violation of our spam policies"; descriptive URLs; anchor text.
  "Things we believe you shouldn't focus on": meta keywords (not used), keyword stuffing, keywords in domain, content length
  (no magical word count), PageRank, duplicate content, heading order, "Thinking E-E-A-T is a ranking factor? No, it's not."
  (list confirmed via secondary reporting of the Feb 2024 update; proxy blocked the direct page body for that section).
  — /search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
