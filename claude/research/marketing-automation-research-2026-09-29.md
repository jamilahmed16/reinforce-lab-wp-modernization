## C. Rules (VERIFIED, primary)
- Gmail sender guidelines (from 1 Feb 2024): all senders SPF or DKIM, PTR, TLS, spam rate < 0.3%; >5,000/day to Gmail:
  SPF + DKIM + DMARC (p=none allowed), From alignment, one-click unsubscribe + visible link. — https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126
- FTC CAN-SPAM: covers all commercial email; "makes no exception for business-to-business email"; physical address;
  honour opt-outs within 10 business days; "you can't contract away your legal responsibility"; penalties up to $53,088 per
  email. — https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
- UK ICO (PECR reg. 22): no marketing email to individuals without specific consent; soft opt-in only for own previous
  customers, not bought-in lists; corporate subscribers may be emailed (keep a do-not-email list). Guidance under review
  after the Data (Use and Access) Act. — ICO electronic mail marketing page.
- Litmus (3 Jun 2024): Apple MPP "accounts for 55% of all opens" as of March 2024; open rate unreliable. —
  https://www.litmus.com/blog/measure-email-marketing-success
