## C. Verified facts
- Patchstack, State of WordPress Security in 2026 (updated 25 Feb 2026): 11,334 new vulnerabilities in 2025 (+42% vs 2024);
  91% plugins, 9% themes, 6 in core; 17% high severity; 46% not fixed by public disclosure; premium components 3× more KEVs;
  median time to mass exploitation for heavily exploited vulns 5 hours; ~half of high-impact vulns exploited within 24 h;
  in a large-scale pentest of hosts only 26% of vulnerability attacks were blocked.
  — https://patchstack.com/whitepaper/state-of-wordpress-security-in-2026/
- WordPress.org Hardening: "Fundamentally, security is not about perfectly secure systems… What security is though is risk
  reduction, not risk elimination"; hosts are responsible for infrastructure, not the application you install.
  — https://developer.wordpress.org/advanced-administration/security/hardening/
- WordPress.org Upgrading: back up first; "Verify the backups you created are there and usable. This is essential."
  — https://wordpress.org/documentation/article/configuring-automatic-background-updates/
