## How the primary was chosen

- The phrase that matches what the post is about, with the best mix of volume and difficulty.
- Review posts target "<product> review", not the product's name: the bare names (rank tracker KD 88, jasper ai KD 77, copy ai, writesonic, surfer seo) are searches for the product's own site.
- Where Semrush shows almost no volume but Search Console shows many impressions, the post keeps the phrase in its address. Semrush undercounts long, specific searches; the impressions are the better evidence.
