## Why these primaries `[RECOMMENDATION]`

- **The export confirmed the H1 names chosen on 11 Nov on 15 of 16 pages.** In each case the "services" phrase matches what the page sells and has a workable difficulty.
- **Executive AI Consulting changed:** "executive ai consulting" has 20 searches a month; "ai consulting services" has 6,600 (KD 50). The H1 small line is now "AI consulting services for executives." The capability name in the menu, label and schema stays Executive AI Consulting (D-023).
- **AI Workflow Automation:** the head term "ai workflow automation" (5,400) is KD 73 and informational; the service phrase (140, KD 37) contains it, so the page targets both. "ai automation services" (1,900, KD 29) is the best secondary.
- **LLM Optimization:** the service phrase is small (90, KD 2) but contains "llm optimization" (880); "llm seo" (1,900) and "llm seo services" (480, KD 11) are covered by a new FAQ.
- **Easiest openings by difficulty:** technical seo agency (3,600, KD 9), off page seo services (2,900, KD 11), llm seo services (480, KD 11), international seo agency (3,600, KD 13), wordpress website design services (1,600, KD 13), international seo services (2,900, KD 17), ai search optimization services (1,000, KD 19, rising every month in the trend column).
- **Hard terms not targeted as primaries:** press release services (KD 100), wordpress website design (KD 100), technical seo audit (KD 80), ai workflow automation (KD 73).
- **Spelling:** Semrush's keyword is "ecommerce"; the site writes "E-commerce" (the locked industry name). Search engines treat the two as the same word, so nothing was changed.

Not in this export, unchanged: Home ("reinforce lab"), AI Growth Systems ("ai growth systems"), core SEO, Website Maintenance.
