## Facts re-checked on official pages (changes to the review drafts)
**Kinsta.** "First month free" for new customers is only available **until 1 December 2026** (kinsta.com/first-month-free). Single 20GB: $0 for the first month, $17.50 a month for 3 months, then $35. Annual saves 2 months. This closes the open end date for Kinsta.
**Cloudways.** Flexible has a **5-day** free trial with no card; Autonomous has a **3-day** trial with no card (pricing page FAQ, home page FAQ and help center). One help article body says "three days" for Flexible while its heading says 5 days, so cite the pricing page. Entry servers: DigitalOcean Standard 1 GB $11, Premium $14 (stackvaluelab, 3 Sep 2026, same as the pricing page). Cloudways is owned by DigitalOcean (Expertah, 10 Nov 2026; Finding the Universe mentions the 2022 takeover). TechRadar (20 Oct 2026): six-minute setup, A on GTmetrix, 903 ms LCP, no conventional money-back guarantee, a WordPress login failed during setup.
**Hostinger.** The $1.99, $2.79 and $7.99 prices on the Black Friday page are **Black Friday prices** on the 48-month term (regular $12.99, $18.99 and $27.99). Gizmodo (30 Sep 2026) lists the pre-sale prices as $2.99, $3.99 and $7.99. The page shows no end date. Renewals $10.99, $16.99, $25.99 confirmed. Premium has weekly backups. Gizmodo: TTFB 291 ms on its second test, PageSpeed 99, Trustpilot 4.7 from 72,699 reviews.
**hosting.com (A2).** A2 was bought by World Host Group on 29 Jan 2025 and renamed hosting.com (announced 28 Apr 2025, complete by 2 May 2025); a2hosting.com now redirects (WPThinker, 6 Nov 2026). Shared Starter $1.49 renews $13.99 (9.4 times); WordPress Small $4.49 renews $9.99; Medium $4.99 renews $17.99. 30-day guarantee; domains, setup and migration fees are not refundable. Website Planet: speeds 0.8 to 2.2 s, 100% uptime over three weeks, support mixed, old A2 branding still showing.
**Nexcess and Liquid Web.** Liquid Web and Nexcess announced they would become one brand on 1 Feb 2024 (press release); nexcess.net began redirecting to liquidweb.com on 7 Oct 2025; then Nexcess was relaunched as the parent brand and the hosting arm renamed "Liquid Web by Nexcess" in April 2026 (sources disagree: 8 April and 21 April; say "April 2026"). nexcess.com now has its own managed WordPress page: Pro from $19, Elite from $29, and it lists **email hosting** in every plan, Monarx malware protection, Visual Comparison, staging and development sites. Older reviews say mailboxes are not included, so confirm on liquidweb.com before stating either way.
**Namecheap.** The EasyWP pricing page shows Starter at $3.24 a month billed yearly (another Namecheap page: $2.91, $34.88 a year, renewing at $48.88 a year), 10 GB NVMe, unlimited bandwidth, 30-day cancel. EasyWP is available on Namecheap or Spaceship. Each EasyWP subscription hosts one site; shared hosting gives cPanel and many sites. Our draft review covers shared Stellar only.
