KD is Semrush difficulty. CPC is a proxy for how much advertisers pay for the click (a sign of commercial value, not our earnings).

## Ranking method
Score = (US volume and CPC) against (KD and how weak the SERP is) and whether our own evidence is ready. Brand-owned SERPs and KD 70 or higher are deferred. Terms Semrush showed with no SERP are flagged.

## Honest limits (INFERENCE)
- A new page usually takes weeks to rank. Pages published 17 to 26 Nov are unlikely to rank for "black friday" terms by 27 Nov. Direct traffic from your own channels, existing pages that already have impressions and the hub's freshness are what can earn during the sale. The pages still matter for December and the next sale.
- The 12-day schedule breaks the earlier pace rules (D-305 and D-306: no two on one date; 4 new posts a week). Options: (1) hold the rules and ship about 5 to 6 pages by 27 Nov, hub first; (2) relax the rules for hosting only, with dates staggered by hour and different days; (3) write all 14 now, publish on a stagger of 2 a day. Needs Jamil's choice.
