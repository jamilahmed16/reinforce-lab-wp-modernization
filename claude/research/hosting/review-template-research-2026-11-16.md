## What the template must do (the standard we build to)
1. Length 2,500 to 3,500 words for a flagship review, plus 8 to 10 FAQs.
2. Above the fold: title, a value-led standfirst, at-a-glance strip (starting price, renewal, money-back, best for), verdict with buy-it-if and skip-it-if, a visit button.
3. Early pricing table with the renewal price next to the sale price, and a total-cost table (our own arithmetic, labelled).
4. A numbers section: independent tests side by side with method and date (never presented as our own).
5. Topic sections every top review covers: speed, uptime, ease of use, AI tools, security, support, upgrade path, WordPress/WooCommerce, comparison table with 3 alternatives, customer sentiment.
6. "Where reviewers agree and differ" table. None of the competitors have one.
7. A "how we researched this review" panel in plain words, not a lab claim. It names the sources, the dates and what we did not do (we did not buy a plan).
8. CTAs at decision points: verdict, after pricing, after pros and cons, after comparison, final verdict, mobile sticky bar. Service CTA after pros and cons.
9. No score and no Review schema unless we hold real test data.
10. Do not use: "evidence log", "the vendor", sourcing apologies in the opening or standfirst.
