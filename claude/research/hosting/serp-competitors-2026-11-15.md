## Observations (INFERENCE from the result lists)
- Big review sites with test data dominate: TechRadar, CNET, HostAdvice, Website Planet, Cybernews, Crazy Egg, All About Cookies, Tom's Hardware. A new page needs original evidence (our tests) or a different angle (the AI features angle, renewal pricing, who should avoid it) to compete.
- "hosting.com review" results already say "formerly A2 Hosting"; users also search "A2 Hosting review". The A2 tutorial (26,373 impressions) should link to the new review.
- "nexcess review" results are titled "Liquid Web (Formerly Nexcess)", confirming the merge in search.
- Several hosts attract strong negative community threads (Network Solutions, Domain.com, Crazy Domains, Spaceship domains, Liquid Web after the takeover). An honest review must cover complaints and cite the review platforms' rating and counts with the date.
- Trustpilot counts seen: Hostinger 11,852; Namecheap 22,231; Kinsta 129; Cloudways 605; ScalaHosting 295; BigScoots 160; Liquid Web 188; Name.com 3,150; Spaceship 661; Crazy Domains AU 7,349; DigitalOcean 589; SSLs.com 104. Re-read live on publish day.
