## B. Regulation & frameworks (VERIFIED, primary)
- European Commission AI Act page (read 29 Sep 2026): risk-based; prohibitions 1–8 effective February 2025 (9th, from
  the AI Omnibus, December 2026); high-risk obligations from 2 December 2027 (e.g. CV-sorting in recruitment); transparency:
  "when using AI systems such as chatbots, humans should be made aware that they are interacting with a machine".
  NOTE: older "2 Aug 2026 general application" dates in secondary sources are superseded for high-risk — not used.
  — https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
- NIST AI RMF 1.0: voluntary; released 26 Jan 2023; being revised under the White House AI Action Plan; functions Govern,
  Map, Measure, Manage (per NIST AI 100-1). — https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
