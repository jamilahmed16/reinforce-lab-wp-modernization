Who we are
[  About Reinforce Lab  ]

We build the system that gets a company found, trusted and chosen.

Reinforce Lab started in Tallinn, Estonia in 2020 as a full-service digital marketing agency. In 2021 we expanded to Bangladesh and built our team in Dhaka, and on 19 April 2022 we founded our company there.

Years of running search, content and marketing for clients showed us the same problem again and again: the work was split across separate tools, separate agencies and a lot of manual effort, and nobody connected the pieces. Today we connect the website, content and search visibility into one system and let AI take over the repetitive work, with a person checking what matters.

Principles
[  Principles  ]

What we stand by.

Our founder is a pharmacist. In pharmaceuticals and healthcare a wrong claim can do harm, so evidence checking is built into how we write for every client.

Claims carry a source, conflicting sources are investigated, and anything unverified goes to a person before it is published.

01

Evidence before claims

We cite our sources and never invent metrics, rankings or results, for our clients or for ourselves.

02

Preserve what already works

Pages that earn traffic, links or trust are protected before anything is redesigned, moved or merged.

03

One system, not separate tactics

Website, content, search and automation are planned together so each one strengthens the others.

04

People review the AI

AI speeds up the work; a person checks what it produces before anything is published or delivered.

05

Measured on business results

We report enquiries, pipeline and revenue next to rankings and traffic.

06

You own the work

Everything we produce for you is yours.
